OUTFIT: This winter couture look is deliberately elegant and minimalist, combining pale cashmere with sharply contrasting black fur. The dominant garment is a long fitted ivory cashmere dress with a high neckline, long sleeves, a narrow waist, and a smooth heavy knit texture that follows the body without clinging excessively. Over the shoulders sits a dramatic cropped black mink jacket with dense low-pile fur, broad rounded shoulders, a wide collar, and short voluminous sleeves. The lower silhouette remains clean and uninterrupted. Accessories consist of black leather gloves, a small black patent-leather handbag, pearl earrings, and pointed black heels. No headwear, allowing the ivory neckline and dark fur collar to remain the focal point. COLOR PALETTE: ivory, black, cool white, pearl.
