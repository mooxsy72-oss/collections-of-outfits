This Central Asian nomadic and Mongolian-inspired winter ensemble features a palette dominated by matte charcoal-black, warm brown fur, and dark brown leather, accented by oxidized silver and turquoise stones, providing full body coverage except for the hands. An ankle-length charcoal-black robe (deel) in heavy matte wool-cotton features a high silver-embroidered mandarin collar and voluminous blouson sleeves ending in wide turquoise-studded silver cuffs and fluffy brown fur trim. Layered over the torso is a sleeveless brown fur vest (uhuu) with a thick shawl collar. A wide dark brown leather belt set with a large circular silver-and-turquoise medallion and hanging silver-tipped straps cinches the waist. A voluminous brown fur hat (malakhai) with a dangling silver-and-turquoise ornament, layered turquoise-set silver chest necklaces, and dark brown leather boots complete the look.
