This traditional boyar-style outfit provides complete coverage using layered garments in an off-white, olive green, gold, and muted red color scheme. The core is an A-line, floor-length sundress-like sarafan made of stiff, patterned jacquard featuring large-scale red and green floral motifs. A vertical gold decorative band inset with oval ruby gems runs down the center front. The loose, sheer white silk under-shirt features voluminous puffed sleeves gathered into long, rigid green and gold embroidered cuffs. Layered over the gown is an olive-green velvet floor-length cape with a wide, rounded gold barmas collar trimmed in brown fur and inset with rubies. Accessories include a white fabric wimple covering the neck, layered pearl necklaces, a rigid cylindrical poyas-crown headdress with gold filigree and pearls, and hidden dark brown leather boots.
