OUTFIT: This rustic winter farming outfit centers on an oversized charcoal wool overshirt with a thick brushed texture, large chest pockets, broad shoulders, and sturdy wooden buttons. Beneath it is a dark brown thermal turtleneck and a thin quilted vest. The lower body consists of wide high-waisted black canvas trousers with reinforced knees and heavy fabric construction. Mid-calf dark brown leather boots with thick wool insulation anchor the outfit. Thick knitted gloves and a weathered brown leather belt provide practical finishing details. The overall silhouette is layered, substantial, and suited to cold barn and field work.
