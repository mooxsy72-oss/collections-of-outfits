This shamanic fantasy ensemble is crafted from an oatmeal-colored, mid-weight woven linen with a slubby texture. The underlayer is a straight-cut, mid-calf tunic with narrow sleeves, leaving the feet completely barefoot. Over it drapes a voluminous, floor-length hooded cloak made from the same fabric, framing the face with an angular hood trimmed in red geometric chevron embroidery.Two thick, loose three-strand braids drape forward over the shoulders down to the waist, adorned near the chest with small animal skulls and bound tightly at the lower third with dried herb sprigs. The chest features layered necklaces combining multi-strand wooden beads, small skulls, and a central long leg bone pendant. A crown of tangled brown twigs, pinecones, and dried pods rests atop the hood.
