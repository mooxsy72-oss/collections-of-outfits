OUTFIT: fantasy-gothic garment blends traditional Chinese cheongsam (qipao) elements with a modern, dramatic high-low silhouette. The entire ensemble follows a strictly monochromatic black color palette, utilizing matte silk, sheer chiffon, and ornate lace.The skin-tight dress features a classic high, stiff mandarin collar that connects to a sheer lace-overlay yoke, creating a chest cutout with criss-cross structural straps. The fitted bodice is constructed from a heavy, smooth matte silk with excellent shape retention. It is adorned with intricate black floral lace appliques trailing down the right shoulder and left hip. The long, tight sleeves are made of ultra-thin, sheer mesh, terminating in ornate lace cuffs.The skirt construction is highly stylized and asymmetric. The front hemline tapers sharply into a dramatic, high-slit V-shape that exposes both upper thighs and hips, framed by intricate scalloped lace trim and side lace-up detailing. Attached underneath the main hem is a lightweight, flowing sheer chiffon train that drapes loosely down to the ankles in sharp, handkerchief points, adding fluid movement. The legs are covered in semi-opaque black thigh-high stockings, paired with classic pointed-toe black leather stiletto pumps.
