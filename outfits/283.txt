This fantasy cocktail dress blends modern asymmetrical tailoring with opulent historical textures, featuring deep burgundy and stark white as equal dominant colors with secondary gold accents. The inner layer is a fitted, short white dress made of lightweight, intricate floral lace over a mesh tulle petticoat, ending mid-thigh. It features a sweetheart neckline, exposed left shoulder, and a sheer lace strap over the right shoulder. Layered diagonally over it is a structured, asymmetrical peplum-style bodice crafted from heavy, stiff burgundy velvet. This structured overlay runs from the right shoulder down to the right hip, bordered by scalloped metallic gold embroidery. The garment leaves the left arm, left shoulder, collarbones, and lower thighs fully exposed, paired with knee-high structured white leather boots featuring chunky block heels and square toes. A double-strand pearl choker sits on the neck. The outfit's focal points are two large, three-dimensional fabric rosettes—one on the right shoulder, one at the right hip—connected by draped multi-strand pearl swags across the chest and below the hip peplum.
