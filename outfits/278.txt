This light pink coquette-style pajama set features a thin, soft cotton-blend fabric with a small, dense all-over print of tiny darker pink bows. The cami top is form-fitting, cropped just above the waist, and has delicate spaghetti shoulder straps. Its deep V-neckline is bordered with white scalloped floral lace trim, leaving the collarbones, shoulders, and arms completely exposed. It is paired with wide-leg pajama pants that feature a loose, fluid drape, full floor length, and a gathered elastic waistband. The voluminous silhouette of the pants dominates the lower half of the outfit, contrasting with the tight-fitting top. A thin gold chain necklace with a small circular pendant is the sole wearable accessory.
