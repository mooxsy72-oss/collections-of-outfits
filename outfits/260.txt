This fantasy angel-inspired costume features a strict, monochrome white palette combining fitted modern tailoring with massive, highly prominent feather structures. The main garment is a form-fitting, sleeveless white mini dress reaching mid-thigh, featuring a round crew neckline and soft horizontal ruching gathered along the side seams of the lightweight, stretchy jersey fabric. The silhouette is heavily dominated by a pair of intentionally exaggerated, oversized white angel wings attached at the back. These wings are exceptionally large and voluminous, constructed from layered, realistic down feathers that extend far above the head and spread wide past the shoulders, completely framing the upper body. The outfit leaves the arms, shoulders, and thighs exposed, paired with knee-high white leather boots featuring a loose, slightly slouchy shaft, thick platform lug soles, and block heels.
