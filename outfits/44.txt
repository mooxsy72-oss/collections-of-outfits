This modern casual streetwear outfit features a dark green and black color palette. The primary layer is a loose-fitting, dark green woolen shacket made from a dense, structured fabric that retains its shape, featuring a classic pointed collar, large patch pockets, and straight full-length sleeves. Underneath, a tight black cropped tank top leaves the midriff exposed. On the lower body, a high-waisted black pleated mini-skirt with wide, sharp knife pleats offers a structured silhouette, leaving the entire length of the legs bare. Accessories include a delicate gold chain necklace with a small green four-leaf clover pendant and matching green stud earrings. Completing the look are sporty black and white low-top sneakers with thick soles and black laces.
