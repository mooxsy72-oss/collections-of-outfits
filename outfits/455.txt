OUTFIT: cozy, casual pajama set features a soft, muted color palette dominated by cream-white and light pastel pink. The ensemble provides near-total body coverage, exposing only the collarbones, shoulders, and a small portion of the lower neck.The top is a relaxed, slightly oversized long-sleeved jersey shirt in solid cream-white. It features a wide, unstructured off-the-shoulder neckline with a short, decorative three-button placket at the center chest. The fabric is a lightweight, fluid ribbed knit with a soft, limp drape and high stretch, falling loosely past the hips.The coordinating pajama pants are oversized, wide-leg trousers in a pale pink fabric adorned with a small-scale, sparsely distributed faded floral pattern. They feature an elasticated waistband woven with a matching pink ribbon drawstring tied at the center front. The pants are made of a lightweight, highly fluid cotton flannel or knit blend that pools slightly around the feet. The visual volume is created entirely by the oversized, slouchy proportions and the soft drape of the unlined fabrics, emphasizing comfort over structure.
