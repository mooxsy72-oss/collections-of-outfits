OUTFIT: monochrome, beige-cream knit ensemble consists of three matching ribbed-knit garments and a chain-strap handbag.The base layers include a body-hugging, floor-length maxi skirt and a matching square-neck crop top that leaves the lower collarbone exposed. Over this sits a fitted, long-sleeve peplum cardigan featuring a deep V-neckline and a three-button front closure. The fabric across all three garments is a medium-weight, heavily textured vertical ribbed knit that provides substantial stretch and body-contouring structure.The outfit is defined by elongated, flared proportions. The cardigan features dramatic, oversized sleeves that extend well past the hands, widening significantly toward the cuffs into a subtle bell shape. A flared, gathered peplum hem springs out from the cardigan's narrow waist, creating a structured, ruffled volume over the upper hips. Below, the high-waisted skirt tapers sharply around the hips and thighs before cascading straight down to the floor, completely covering the legs and feet.A pale pink, structured rectangular shoulder bag with a metallic gold chain strap rests against the lower hip, serving as the sole accessory and adding a slight color contrast to the dominant beige palette.
