OUTFIT: This rugged survival outfit is inspired by traditional Siberian trapping clothing but refined into a powerful cinematic silhouette. The dominant garment is a heavy knee-length brown leather coat with thick grey shearling lining, broad shoulders, oversized sleeves, a high collar, and large front pockets. Beneath it is a fitted dark charcoal sweater and a rugged black leather vest. The lower body consists of extremely thick charcoal trousers with leather reinforcement around the knees and calves, tucked into oversized brown felt-lined boots. A wide leather belt, canvas satchel, heavy gloves, and several aged metal fasteners complete the outfit. COLOR PALETTE: dark brown, charcoal, black leather, grey shearling, muted metal.
