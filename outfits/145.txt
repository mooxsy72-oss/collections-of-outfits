This fantasy Russian boyar-inspired ensemble features a dominant ivory-white palette with secondary gold, light blue, and deep red accents. Providing full body coverage, the outfit centers on a floor-length, flared gown made of mid-weight ivory damask fabric with a tonal floral weave. It features a wide, circular barmy collar and a matching central vertical trim band, both heavily detailed with gold embroidery, pearls, and red cabochon gems. The gown has long, slit cape-like hanging sleeves lined with gold lace trim, revealing voluminous light blue under-sleeves woven with gold floral damask patterns and fitted into tight, pearl-encrusted gold cuffs. A slender gold cord belt wraps the waist. A massive, pointed kokoshnik headdress in matching gold and pearl filigree dominates the upper silhouette. The look is completed by matching pointed-toe pumps crafted from ivory damask and accented with gold lace trim, peeking slightly from the hem.
