OUTFIT: This Russian-inspired winter couture look combines enormous ivory sheepskin volume with deep emerald velvet. The dominant garment is a full-length oversized shuba made from exceptionally thick dense ivory sheepskin with a soft plush surface and slightly irregular natural texture. The silhouette is dramatically oversized and rounded, with broad shoulders, an enormous folded collar, wide sleeves, and heavy cuffs. Beneath the coat is a fitted deep emerald-green velvet dress with an extremely dense matte surface and subtle directional sheen. The dress has a high neckline, long fitted sleeves, a narrow waist, and a long flowing skirt that contrasts with the immense volume of the coat. A narrow antique-gold belt is positioned high at the waist. The headwear is a rounded ivory fur hat with a deep emerald velvet band matching the dress. Accessories consist of large warm ivory pearl earrings and a small antique-gold brooch shaped like a restrained floral ornament. Footwear consists of dark espresso leather pointed boots with a narrow heel. The overall palette is ivory, deep emerald, espresso brown, and antique gold, creating Russian winter fairy-tale luxury, aristocratic elegance, enormous soft sheepskin, and jewel-toned velvet.
