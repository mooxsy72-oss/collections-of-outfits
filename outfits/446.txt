OUTFIT: This post-apocalyptic outfit is designed for traveling through ash-covered frozen landscapes, combining a long protective silhouette with rugged survival materials. The dominant garment is an oversized blackened charcoal parka reaching to the knees, made from thick matte waxed canvas with a rigid high collar, broad shoulders, large storm pockets, and dark brown leather reinforcement along the arms. Beneath it is a fitted dark grey wool sweater and a heavy leather harness. The lower body consists of loose black cargo trousers tucked into massive black leather boots with thick insulated soles. Heavy gloves, a weathered cross-body satchel, and minimal steel hardware complete the look. COLOR PALETTE: blackened charcoal, dark brown, graphite grey, black leather.
