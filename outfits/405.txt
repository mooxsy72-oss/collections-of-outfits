OUTFIT: Y2K-inspired casual ensemble coordinates an off-the-shoulder cream top with ultra-low-rise denim shorts and chunky black platform boots. The fitted, asymmetric rib-knit top features a flexible, medium-weight texture that clings to the torso before flaring into a split, lettuce-edge hemline. It features an elasticated off-the-shoulder neckline exposing the collarbones and shoulders, long sleeves that flare into wide bell cuffs, and a decorative, oversized satin bow pinned near the collarbone.The dark-wash grey denim micro-shorts feature an extreme low-rise waistband with a button closure, leaving the entire midriff and upper hip area exposed. The fabric is thick and rigid with subtle horizontal whiskering across the front. Draped from the right belt loop is a prominent black star-shaped chain charm.The lower leg is stylized with thick knit, slouchy leg warmers featuring alternating horizontal stripes of charcoal grey and black, detailed with large embroidered silver stars. The footwear consists of heavy black leather combat boots with thick platform soles, chunky block heels, front lacing, and a silver gothic cross embellishment on the outer shaft. Layered silver chains and a black choker necklace complete the accessories.
