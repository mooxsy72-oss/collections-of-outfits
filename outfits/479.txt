OUTFIT: This softer autumn gardening outfit combines traditional knitwear with sturdy work garments. The dominant upper layer is a thick oversized charcoal wool cardigan with chunky cable-knit texture, large wooden buttons, dropped shoulders, and deep patch pockets. Beneath it is a simple muted sage-green cotton shirt with a high rounded neckline. The lower body consists of straight-cut dark brown canvas trousers with a high waist and reinforced seat and knees. A long faded olive work apron is worn over the trousers, reaching to just above the knees and featuring several large practical pockets across the front. The feet are covered by dark burgundy leather ankle boots with thick rubber soles, while beige knitted gloves complete the outfit. The overall silhouette is warm, slightly oversized, tactile, and distinctly rural without looking shabby.
