This Y2K-inspired casual outfit features a solid, tri-color palette of white, light blue denim, and metallic silver without patterns. The secondary upper piece is a tight, cropped white tube top made of smooth, stretchy jersey knit that fully exposes the shoulders, collarbones, and midriff. The dominant lower piece is an ultra-short denim micro-mini skirt constructed from thick, stiff light-wash blue cotton denim. The skirt's structure relies on an intentionally extreme, exaggerated design featuring two oversized, crisscrossing belts with prominent, rectangular metallic buckles that wrap around the low hips, creating asymmetric geometric volume. The statement footwear consists of metallic silver stiletto sandals featuring slender straps that wrap in tight spirals all the way up the calves.
