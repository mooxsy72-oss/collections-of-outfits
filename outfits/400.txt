OUTFIT: airy, modern ensemble features a uniform color palette dominated entirely by shades of light blue, subtly balanced through contrasting textures and structures.The main upper layer is a lightweight, semi-sheer chiffon blouse with a subtle cloudy tie-dye pattern in pale blue and white. It has an empire waist silhouette that exposes the center chest with an intentionally deep, plunging V-neckline. The front is fastened at the under-bust with a large, fluid fabric tie that hangs down into long, asymmetrical cascading ruffles. The blouse features voluminous bishop sleeves with gathered elastic cuffs, creating a breezy, draped volume that contrasts with the fitted lower half.On the bottom are high-waisted, form-fitting jeans made of smooth, light-wash denim. They hug the hips and thighs snugly down to the calves, where they open into subtle flares. The outer side seams of the lower legs feature decorative lace-up detailing with matching denim strings tied at the ankle hems. Open-toe white strappy heeled sandals with thin horizontal bands over the toes complete the look. Wearable accessories include a classic gold metal watch on the left wrist.
