OUTFIT: This outfit belongs to a lone Siberian nomad adapted to permanent nuclear winter, combining enormous natural materials with a surprisingly elegant silhouette. The dominant garment is a massive dark grey wool coat reaching almost to the ankles, layered with brown sheepskin panels along the shoulders, collar, and cuffs. The coat has a broad silhouette, oversized sleeves, and a heavy drape. Beneath it is a fitted black wool sweater and loose dark trousers tucked into tall weathered leather boots. A broad leather belt, cross-body satchel, thick gloves, and several small metal survival tools complete the outfit. COLOR PALETTE: charcoal grey, dark brown, black, natural sheepskin, aged steel.
