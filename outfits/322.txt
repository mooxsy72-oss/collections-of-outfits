This ice-fantasy evening gown features a monochromatic light icy-blue color distribution, heavily embellished with shimmering silver tones and no printed patterns.
The gown is a floor-length mermaid silhouette crafted from structured, mid-weight translucent tulle over an icy-blue lining, contouring tightly through the bodice, waist, and hips before flaring into a soft pool at the floor. It features an off-the-shoulder, sweetheart neckline that leaves the collarbones and shoulders exposed.
The visual volume is dominated by an oversized, bulky trim of light blue feathers or pointed fabric petals wrapped entirely around the shoulders and bust. The entire body of the gown is covered in a dense, vertical organic pattern of glittering silver embroidery, beads, and crystals that create a frozen, crystalline texture down to the hem.
