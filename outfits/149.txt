This fantasy Russian boyar-inspired ensemble features a dominant rose-pink and gold palette with cream secondary accents. Providing full body coverage, the floor-length, A-line opashon coat is made of structured rose-pink damask fabric with large woven gold floral brocade patterns. It features a wide decorative gold border down the center front split and long, slit cape-like hanging sleeves. Peeking out from underneath are the voluminous, gathered cream satin sleeves of an under-shirt, fitted into tight cuffs. A massive, stiff crescent-shaped kokoshnik headdress dominates the silhouette, heavily encrusted with pearls, gold filigree, and colorful cabochon gems, featuring long dangling pearl strands. A large, circular golden barmy collar set with pearls rests over the shoulders. Rounded brown leather shoes are minimally visible at the hem.
