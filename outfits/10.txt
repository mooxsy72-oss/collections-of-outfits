OUTFIT: floor-length ensemble draws heavily from Russian and Slavic aristocratic historical attire, resembling a luxury shuba coat worn over a formal gown. The color palette is dominated by deep black, rich burgundy, and metallic gold accents.The primary outer layer is a tailored, structured A-line overcoat made of thick, heavy black wool or velvet that provides high shape retention and drapes in stiff, clean lines. It features a deep V-neckline framed by a plush, dark brown fur collar, padded shoulders, and long, slim sleeves. Five large, polished gold dome buttons secure the upper torso, while the coat splits open from the waist to the floor. Dense, symmetrical gold and burgundy floral embroidery adorns the upper chest, lower sleeves, and the outer bottom hems.Beneath the coat is a floor-length gown crafted from a mid-weight, patterned burgundy damask or brocade fabric. It features a subtle V-neckline and provides full torso and leg coverage. Metallic gold pointed-toe slippers peek from beneath the hem, and filigree gold drop earrings provide a delicate secondary focal point.
