This Tibetan nomadic-inspired winter folk ensemble (chuba robe and pangden apron) features a rich palette dominated by deep crimson-red wool, pure white curly shearling, and matte charcoal-black, accented with multicolored geometric stripes and turquoise jewelry, providing full body coverage except for the hands. Dominating the silhouette is an oversized, calf-length asymmetrical wrap coat (chuba) in heavy matte crimson wool with dropped beaded shoulder seams, exaggeratedly wide bell sleeves, and thick white shearling lining bordering the standing collar, diagonal overlapping lapel, vertical front edge, and deep sleeve cuffs. Wide woven bands of rust, beige, and black geometric chevrons decorate the lower sleeves and form a central front pangden apron cinched by a matching striped waist sash with a hanging gray-brown fur tail pelt. A flowing ankle-length black underskirt, layered turquoise-and-coral bead necklaces, and an ornate silver forehead chain headdress complete the look.
