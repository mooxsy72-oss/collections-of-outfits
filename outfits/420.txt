OUTFIT: This high-fashion winter look relies on immense physical volume, pairing a dramatic, sprawling outerwear piece with a minimalist underlayer. The dominant garment is a floor-length shuba style coat crafted from dense, premium dark sable-brown mink fur. The coat features an intentionally oversized, flared silhouette with wide, horizontal tiered skin paneling along the sleeves and upper body. It has a massive, structured notch lapel collar that extends broad across the shoulders, and exaggeratedly wide, elongated bell sleeves that pool over the hands, creating heavy visual mass.Beneath the open coat, a tight, form-fitting olive-green turtleneck sweater provides full neck and torso coverage. The knit fabric is smooth and lightweight, contrasting with the thick, plush fur. Accessories are minimal, consisting only of a dark brown structured leather clutch bag held in one hand by a thin, matching leather wrist strap.
