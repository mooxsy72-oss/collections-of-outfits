This monochrome ensemble relies completely on solid matte and semi-gloss black textures, creating a uniform, dark color palette across all layers where visual attention is driven by the stark contrast between the heavy, oversized upper proportions and the form-fitting silhouette of the lower legs. An underlying matte black knit dress serves as the base layer, featuring a high, tight turtleneck collar and a very short hemline, cinched at the natural waist by a narrow black leather belt with a small silver buckle. Over this sits an exaggerated, oversized black leather jacket with dropped shoulders, loose voluminous sleeves, a high-reaching funnel collar secured with a throat latch, a wide asymmetrical front flap, and an elasticized ribbed leather hemline resting low on the hips. The legs are covered by sheer black hosiery and wide-shaft, over-the-knee boots in soft black leather that rise to the mid-thigh, featuring a slouchy, unstructured silhouette that wrinkles around the ankles, a squared-off toe box, and low, chunky block heels, accented only by small, rounded silver button earrings.
