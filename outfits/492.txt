OUTFIT: This practical winter farm ensemble is built around a heavily padded dark brown sleeveless vest with a long hip-covering silhouette, oversized collar, deep pockets, and weather-resistant canvas exterior. Beneath it is a thick sage-green wool sweater with a high ribbed neck, layered over a thermal shirt. The lower body consists of loose cream-brown insulated trousers with reinforced knees and wide cuffs tucked into dark leather winter boots. Thick wool gloves and a compact leather tool belt complete the outfit. The combination of padded vest and oversized knitwear creates warmth without excessive restriction during physical work.
