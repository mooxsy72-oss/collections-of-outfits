OUTFIT: three-piece pinstriped suit balances a sharply tailored, cropped upper body with elongated, high-volume lower proportions. The color palette is grounded in a warm beige dominant tone with dark brown vertical pinstripes, contrasted by a secondary light blue shirt and a patterned brown tie.The look begins with a long-sleeved, light blue button-down shirt featuring a sharp, oversized pointed collar and extended cuffs. Over it sits a dark brown silk tie with an intricate paisley pattern. Layered above is a structured, asymmetrical waistcoat with an overlapping front, fastened diagonally by three metallic gold buttons near the cropped hemline. A matching ultra-cropped jacket sits on top, featuring structured shoulders, classic notched lapels, and long, straight sleeves that allow the blue shirt cuffs to peek through.The high-waisted, form-fitting trousers hug the hips and thighs before flaring dramatically from the knee down into voluminous, floor-sweeping bell-bottoms. The mid-weight fabric provides high shape retention, maintaining crisp lines on the structured tailoring while allowing a structured drape at the wide hems. Underneath the trousers, the outfit is completed by metallic gold pointed-toe heeled shoes.
