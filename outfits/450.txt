OUTFIT: This post-apocalyptic, utilitarian survival outfit features a strictly monochromatic, slate-gray and charcoal color palette with total body coverage.The dominant element is a structured, floor-length duster coat made of heavy, weathered shearling or distressed suede, trimmed with a plush, dense fur lining along the open placket and notched lapels. This thick outer layer sits over a loose, elongated, heavy-gauge knit sweater with a high mock-neck collar, which is cinched tightly at the waist by an adjustable tactical webbing belt with a rectangular metallic utility buckle. The lower half consists of relaxed-fit, wide-leg gray cargo trousers made from a mid-weight, matte technical fabric that stacks heavily over chunky, thick-soled black combat boots.The primary accessory is a large, slouchy, multi-pocketed nylon tactical duffel bag with utilitarian webbing straps. Visual volume is driven by the dense, unyielding structure of the shearling coat and the bulk of the heavy knitwear underneath, creating an exaggerated, protective, and heavily layered silhouette that completely obscures the natural human form.
