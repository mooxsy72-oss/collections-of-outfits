This fantasy bridal evening gown fuses modern high-fashion tailoring with romantic, voluminous accents in a monochromatic palette of stark, uniform white. The fitted bodice is a sheer, structured corset with a sweetheart neckline and distinct, vertical white boning channels. Dramatically oversized, voluminous cold-shoulder sleeves are constructed from heavily ruffled, layered tulle, blooming into massive three-dimensional rosette shapes at the upper arms. The floor-length, asymmetric skirt features tight diagonal gathering across the hips, opening into a high thigh-length slit on the right side. The slit edge is bordered by a cascading waterfall of dense, tiered tulle ruffles that flow down into a sweeping floor-length train. The garment leaves the shoulders, upper chest, arms, and right leg exposed, paired with classic white pointed-toe patent leather pumps. Wearable accessories include a multi-strand pearl choker necklace with a central crystal drop pendant, a large statement silver cocktail ring with an oval pearl center, and a matching silver band ring.
