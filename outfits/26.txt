This Tibetan traditional-inspired winter festival ensemble (chuba robe with pangden apron) features a rich palette dominated by matte jet black, warm golden-tan fur, and terracotta rust, accented with white, slate blue, and multicolored embroidery, providing full body coverage except for the hands and neck. Dominating the silhouette is a voluminous, calf-length asymmetrical wrap coat (chuba) in heavy black and rust-red woven wool, featuring exaggeratedly wide, elongated bell sleeves and a thick, plush golden-tan fur border tracing the diagonal front lapel, massive sleeve cuffs, and lower hem. Cinched at the waist by a fur-trimmed belt, a central black apron panel displays dense multicolored dragon-and-cloud embroidery framed by geometric stripes, four white tassels, and polychrome cloud-wave hem patterns. Worn over a white high-neck blouse, tan mid-calf boots, and a beaded Tibetan forehead headdress with side tassels complete the look.
