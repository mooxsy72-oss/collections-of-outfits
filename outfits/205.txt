This modern, cottagecore-inspired summer ensemble relies on a palette of solid white and cream with a delicate floral print. The dominant lower layer is a high-waisted mini skirt featuring an elasticized waistband with a small center label, opening into structured box pleats that create an exaggerated, high-volume flared silhouette. It is crafted from a crisp, mid-weight cream woven cotton or linen fabric printed with a sparse, dark charcoal botanical branch motif. Tucked into the skirt is a tight, form-fitting white tube top made of smooth, stretchy jersey knit that fully exposes the shoulders, arms, and collarbones. A matching triangular headscarf, made from the exact same cream floral fabric, is tied around the hair, completing the coordinated look.
