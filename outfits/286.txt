This casual, hyper-feminine streetwear outfit features a monochromatic pink color distribution composed of a delicate lace base, a sporty oversized layer, and glossy accessories.
The core garment is a fitted, mid-thigh-length light pink slip dress made of lightweight sheer floral lace with a scalloped hemline and a straight neckline. Layered over it is an exaggeratedly oversized pink windbreaker jacket in lightweight, slightly crinkled nylon, featuring a high stand-up collar, a gathered elastic hem, and exceptionally voluminous balloon sleeves with elasticated cuffs. The lower legs are covered by sheer, thigh-high light pink stockings with solid, tight-fitting top bands.
On the feet are glossy pale pink patent leather pumps with pointed toes. Wearable accessories include a structured pink patent leather mini shoulder bag with a silver chain strap, symmetrical gold drop earrings, and a thin red cord bracelet on the right wrist.
