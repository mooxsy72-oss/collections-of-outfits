OUTFIT: outfit is a monochome, evening wear ensemble composed entirely of solid creamy ivory and stark white elements. Below the waist, the skirt drapes vertically, parting on the wearer's right side into a prominent, structural thigh-high leg slit.Draped over the shoulders is a thick, highly voluminous stole made of plush white faux fur, serving as the outer layer. It wraps loosely around the upper arms, creating exaggerated, rounded shapes that counter the column-like silhouette of the dress.On the feet are matching pointed-toe stiletto pumps made from semi-matte ivory fabric, featuring delicate, slim ankle straps with metallic buckles.The look is completed with several wearable accessories: a pair of matching opera-length satin gloves that extend smoothly past the elbows; a delicate, short strand pearl necklace fitted closely at the base of the neck; and a small, structured quilted leather flap handbag in a matching cream tone, accented by a woven gold chain top handle.
