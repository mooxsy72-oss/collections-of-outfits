OUTFIT: This winter ensemble draws directly from historical Soviet-era military and utilitarian aesthetics, featuring thick, protective layers in a monochromatic earth-toned palette of muted khaki, olive, and brown.The dominant outer layer is a heavy, knee-length double-breasted overcoat, resembling a classic wool shinel. It features a straight, tailored fit, dark contrasting buttons, and a wide notch lapel collar lined with textured brown sheepskin fur. The fabric is a coarse, thick woolen cloth with a dense weave that maintains rigid structure. Beneath the open coat, a high-collared olive-green knit turtleneck sweater provides total chest and neck coverage, paired with loose-fitting, matching olive wool utility trousers.Footwear consists of traditional Russian valenki felt boots in a mottled charcoal-grey hue, showing a stiff, seamless construction and wide, unstructured shafts. A ribbed, slouchy tan knit beanie covers the head, while heavy, padded brown leather mittens protect the hands.
