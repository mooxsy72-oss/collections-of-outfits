This fantasy Russian boyar-inspired ensemble features a dominant burgundy and gold palette paired with cream and multicolored tapestry accents. Providing full body coverage, the outfit centers on a floor-length, A-line sarafan dress made of heavy, structured burgundy brocade woven with a large gold floral damask pattern, featuring a buttoned central gold trim band. Underneath is a cream satin shirt with voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into tight, gold-embroidered cuffs. Layered on top is a heavy, floor-length open cape lined with dark brown fabric, boasting an outer shell of multicolored floral tapestry cloth and trimmed with plush, oversized white faux-fur rolls at the collar and hem. A stiff, crescent-shaped kokoshnik headdress in matching burgundy and gold brocade completes the silhouette, while matching closed-toe dark brown leather shoes are entirely concealed beneath the sweeping hemline.
