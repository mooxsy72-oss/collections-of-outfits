OUTFIT: outfit reflects a traditional Slavic or early medieval European peasant aesthetic, supported by the embroidered tunic, fabric belt pouch, and characteristic leg wraps. The color palette is earthy and monochromatic, dominated by natural beige and off-white tones on the inner layers, contrasted by a muted grayish-brown on the outer vest and lower-body elements.The upper body features an off-white, light-to-medium weight linen tunic with a loose fit, reaching mid-thigh. It features a split-neck collar and long, straight sleeves gathered slightly at the wrists. Geometry is defined by straight hems with dark brown geometric embroidery bordering the neck slit, cuffs, and bottom hem. Layered over this is a sleeveless, thigh-length textured wool vest in a desaturated grayish-brown, featuring an open front, straight edges, and raw, frayed hems that expose the lighter sleeves beneath.For the lower body, voluminous, loose-fitting trousers in a matching grayish-brown fabric are gathered at the calves. From the upper calf down to the ankles, the legs are tightly encased in light-colored linen cloth wraps, secured crisscross style with thin brown leather cords. This tight wrapping compresses the lower leg, contrasting with the billowing trousers above.Accessories include a dark brown leather tie-belt cinching the waist, with a small, rounded brown fabric pouch suspended from it on the left side. The footwear consists of simple, ankle-high brown leather shoes with a rounded toe and low heels.
