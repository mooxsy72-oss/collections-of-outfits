OUTFIT: This outfit takes abandoned Soviet industrial workwear and elevates it into a brutal high-end winter survival aesthetic. The dominant garment is a huge dark brown shearling work coat with thick dense fur, a heavy structured collar, broad shoulders, oversized sleeves, leather reinforcement panels, and large functional pockets. Beneath it is a fitted charcoal merino-style sweater with a high neck and a dark leather vest. The lower body consists of wide blackened canvas trousers with reinforced knees, thick suspenders, and heavy stacking above massive black leather work boots. A wide leather belt with a steel buckle, rugged gloves, and a heavy canvas shoulder strap complete the silhouette. COLOR PALETTE: dark brown, black, charcoal, aged leather, steel.
