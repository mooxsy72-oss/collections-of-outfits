OUTFIT: ankle-length ensemble blends contemporary knitwear with a Scottish Highlands aesthetic, defined by its prominent tartan maxi skirt and layered textures. The color scheme is moody and autumnal, dominated by charcoal gray, forest green, navy blue, and slate gray.The base layer is a fitted, medium-weight knit sweater in slate gray with long sleeves that extend past the wrists into ribbed, fingerless-style cuffs. Layered over the torso is a thick, bulky charcoal gray knit wrap shawl. This wrap crosses over the chest in an X-shape, creating a high, clustered V-neckline while tightly hugging the shoulders and empire waist. The wrap features a heavy, raised cable-knit pattern that introduces significant visual volume to the upper body.The lower half is anchored by a structured, floor-sweeping kilt-style maxi skirt. Crafted from a thick, heavy woolen fabric that holds dense box pleats, it flares from a fitted waistband into an A-line silhouette. The skirt features a large-scale, repeating plaid tartan pattern composed of intersecting forest green, navy blue, and gray stripes. Simple, dark round-toed leather shoes subtly peek out from beneath the hem. The outfit offers complete body coverage, leaving no skin exposed below the chin.
