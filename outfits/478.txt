OUTFIT: This wet-weather dacha ensemble is designed for cold rain and muddy paths, with a clean utilitarian silhouette. The dominant garment is an oversized matte navy waterproof raincoat reaching to mid-thigh, featuring a large hood, high zipped collar, dropped shoulders, sealed seams, adjustable cuffs, and deep front pockets. Beneath it is a thick heather-beige fleece pullover with a short stand collar and dense soft texture. The lower body consists of relaxed dark brown work trousers made from heavy water-resistant canvas, with reinforced knees and a straight leg. Tall matte-black rubber boots with deep tread anchor the outfit, while charcoal waterproof gloves and a small olive canvas backpack provide practical accessories. The silhouette is deliberately loose and protective, with the raincoat forming the largest visual layer.
