This medieval European and fantasy-inspired layered ensemble features a monochromatic deep burgundy-plum palette accented with muted bronze-gold and charcoal embroidery, providing complete body coverage except for the hands. Beneath the outerwear sits a fitted, floor-length burgundy wool-crepe gown with a structured V-shaped basque waist, a densely gathered, voluminous A-line skirt, and slim full-length sleeves ending in wide dark charcoal-and-bronze geometric embroidered cuffs. Layered over the shoulders is an open-front, elbow-length structured burgundy capelet bordered along its front edges and hem with wide bronze-gold geometric and botanical folk embroidery. Topping the look is a voluminous, draped burgundy wool headscarf wrapped loosely around the head and neck in thick folds, edged with matching bronze-gold geometric embroidery.
