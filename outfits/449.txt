OUTFIT: cold-weather ensemble blends post-apocalyptic utility with traditional Slavic winter elements, featuring a monochromatic, deep olive-brown and gray palette with full body coverage.The primary silhouette is defined by an oversized, knee-length down puffer coat. It features wide horizontal quilting, a high-zippered stand collar, a voluminous hood, and straight, bulky sleeves. The fabric is a smooth, matte nylon with high loft and structural shape retention. Beneath, a thick, dark brown ribbed-knit sweater layers over loose-fitting, relaxed cargo trousers that taper dramatically into elasticated hems.On the head sits a traditional ushanka hat made from dense, plush dark brown faux fur with the earflaps tied down. A long, dark brown ribbed knit scarf with fine fringed edges wraps tightly around the neck. The hands are covered in thick, dark brown knit mittens. The footwear consists of traditional mid-calf valenki—seamless, structured boots made of thick, rigid heather-gray felted wool with flat, rounded toes. Visual volume is heavily driven by the insulated padding of the puffer coat and the dense, unyielding structure of the felted boots.
