It features a keyhole split neckline without a collar, straight full-length sleeves, and a continuous waist. The fabric is a medium-weight, coarsely textured linen or wool with soft drape and low shape retention. Woven geometric trim borders the neckline, cuffs, and bottom hem.TrousersBeneath the tunic, loose trousers provide voluminous shape around the thighs and knees. Made from a heavy, matte-textured dark grey-brown woven fabric, they are gathered at the lower leg.Leg Wraps & FootwearThe lower legs are tightly bound from ankle to below the knee with dark grey fabric bands, crisscrossed with brown cord. Simple, ankle-high dark brown leather boots with a matte finish sit beneath the wraps.AccessoriesA wide, tablet-woven textile sash in red and brown geometric patterns wraps the waist, its long fringed ends hanging down the front.
