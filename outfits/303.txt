This classic retro-inspired autumn outfit features a dominant black and brown color palette with an elegant A-line silhouette. The primary layer is a structured, sleeveless black mini shift dress made from a smooth, mid-weight woven fabric with strong shape retention, featuring a high crew neckline and a slightly flared hem. Layered over it is a secondary, oversized brown tweed blazer jacket crafted from a heavy wool-blend fabric with a dense houndstooth check pattern, prominent notched lapels, and flap pockets. Semitranslucent sheer black tights fully cover the legs down to the feet. On the feet are glossy black patent leather Mary Jane pumps featuring a squared toe, a thick low block heel, and a slim instep strap.
