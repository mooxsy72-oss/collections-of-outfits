OUTFIT: ensemble heavily draws from historical 17th-century Ukrainian Cossack attire, defined by the distinctive wide trousers, traditional linen shirt, heavy winter cloak, and extensive weapons belt. The silhouette is exceptionally bulky and voluminous, driven by the gathered fabric of the trousers, wide sleeves, and a heavy fur-lined cloak. The dominant colors are dark charcoal gray, deep wine red, and off-white, heavily accented by brown leather and steel armor plates.The base layer is an off-white linen shirt (sorochka) featuring a loose fit, gathered drop-shoulder sleeves with narrow cuffs, and a split crew neckline secured with a thin lace. Over the shirt sits a massive, heavy cloak (kopieniak) made of thick, coarse charcoal wool, lined entirely with textured brown sheepskin fur that rolls over the front lapels.The lower body is defined by exaggeratedly voluminous, baggy trousers (sharovary) in a deep wine-red woven wool that drapes in deep, heavy folds. These are tucked into sturdy, calf-high leather boots with a wrinkled texture.The waist is heavily reinforced with a multi-layered leather belt system, including a wide, segmented steel-plated belt worn over a wrapped brown fabric sash. This rig supports multiple leather holster pouches, an axe loop, and a powder horn, completely covering the midriff and hips. Steel vambraces with leather trim protect the forearms. The outfit offers total body coverage apart from the upper chest.
