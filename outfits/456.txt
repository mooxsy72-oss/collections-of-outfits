OUTFIT: two-piece lounge set features a romantic, cottagecore-inspired aesthetic in a solid, monochromatic stark white. The heavily cropped set exposes the entire midriff, shoulders, collarbones, upper chest, and thighs.The top is an ultra-cropped, off-the-shoulder peasant-style blouse. It features a straight, elasticated neckline and a matching elasticated hem, both creating short, gathered frill trims. The short, voluminous puff sleeves are gathered at the shoulders and upper arms with encased elastic. The coordinating high-waisted shorts feature an elasticated waistband with a gathered ruffle top. The shorts drape into two layers of overlapping, structured ruffles that flare outward across the hips.Both garments are constructed from lightweight, crisp cotton eyelet fabric, densely covered in a small-scale geometric and floral openwork embroidery pattern. The stiff embroidered textile provides high shape retention, holding the airy volume of the puffed sleeves and tiered ruffles without collapsing.
