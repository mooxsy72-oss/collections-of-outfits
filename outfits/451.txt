OUTFIT: modern streetwear ensemble combines athleisure with avant-garde structure, featuring a strictly monochromatic jet-black color palette with minimal white accents. The outfit offers total body coverage from head to toe, leaving no skin exposed.The silhouette is defined by starkly contrasting proportions. The upper body features a heavily cropped, hybrid corset-zip hoodie. The top portion is made from thick, structured cotton fleece with an oversized hood and voluminous, slouchy balloon sleeves tapering into dense ribbed cuffs. This transitions into a stiff, tightly fitted corset panel at the waist, creating a sharp, exaggerated hourglass shape. The lower half consists of extremely oversized, pooling track pants in a lightweight, crinkled nylon windbreaker fabric. They feature an elasticated drawstring waist, a small white graphic logo on the left thigh, and double white piping running down the sides, completely enveloping the chunky white-soled sneakers beneath.Accessories include a matte canvas baseball cap with integrated side-ear flaps and a slouchy, oversized black hobo-style shoulder bag made of soft, fluid cotton fabric. The visual volume is created by the dramatic interplay between the rigid, shape-retaining corset waist and the loose, billowing, draped fabrics of the sleeves and trousers.
