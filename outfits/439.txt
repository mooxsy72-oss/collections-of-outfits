OUTFIT: This post-apocalyptic hunting outfit combines brutal Arctic survival gear with understated luxury and a strong masculine silhouette. The dominant garment is a huge dark grey fur-lined hunting coat made from heavy weatherproof canvas, with broad shoulders, a high protective collar, oversized sleeves, reinforced leather panels, and deep utility pockets. Beneath it is a fitted black thermal turtleneck and a dark brown wool vest. The lower body consists of heavy charcoal trousers with leather knee panels and thick cuffs tucked into tall black-brown leather winter boots. A wide leather belt and chest harness carry compact utility pouches, while thick black gloves complete the look. COLOR PALETTE: dark grey, black, brown leather, muted beige fur.
