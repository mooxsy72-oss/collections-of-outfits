OUTFIT: This Russian-inspired high-fashion winter look is centered entirely around deep wine tones and luxurious tactile contrast. The dominant garment is an oversized mid-calf shuba made from thick long-haired fox fur in a dark oxblood-brown shade with subtle burgundy variation throughout the pile. The coat has immense rounded volume, a dramatic oversized collar, broad shoulders, and wide sleeves with large fluffy cuffs. Underneath is a fitted deep plum velvet dress with a dense matte surface and soft directional sheen, following the body closely from the shoulders to the hips before falling into a long narrow skirt. A dark burgundy leather belt creates a subtle waistline. The headwear is a large rounded oxblood fur hat matching the coat. Jewelry consists only of deep garnet earrings set in muted antique gold. Footwear consists of dark burgundy patent leather pointed pumps. The overall palette is oxblood, deep plum, dark burgundy, and antique gold, creating Russian winter aristocracy, dark wine glamour, luxurious fur, rich velvet, and dramatic monochromatic couture.
