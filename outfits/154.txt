This Eastern European and medieval royal winter-inspired fantasy ensemble features a rich palette dominated by deep burgundy-crimson velvet, plush pure white fur, and metallic gold embroidery, providing complete body coverage except for the hands. Beneath the outerwear sits a fitted, ankle-length burgundy velvet gown with a high, gold-piped standing mandarin collar and slim, wrist-length sleeves. Dense, symmetrical gold baroque floral scrollwork embellishes the center bodice and repeats as an ornate border along the lower skirt hem. Dominating the silhouette is a voluminous, floor-sweeping burgundy velvet coronation cloak with structured elbow-length capelet shoulders, completely edged with thick, fluffy white fur trim and fastened at the throat by an ornate gold-and-ruby brooch. Dark brown pointed-toe shoes, a twisted gold cuff bracelet, and an intricate gold-and-ruby filigree tiara complete the look.
