This modern summer look is defined by structured volume and a solid, bi-color palette of sage green and cream. The secondary upper layer is a tight, form-fitting olive-green tank top in stretch jersey knit, featuring a square neckline, wide shoulder straps, and dense horizontal ruched draping across the torso. It is tucked into the dominant lower layer: high-waisted, cream-colored structured shorts made from a thick, shape-retaining woven canvas or heavy crepe. These shorts feature a clean waistband and architectural, layered petal-like pleats that flare out dynamically, exposing the entire leg. Accessories include a small, structured sage-green quilted handbag with a wide fabric shoulder strap and metallic gold charms, paired with minimalist, metallic gold stiletto sandals featuring slender diagonal foot straps.
