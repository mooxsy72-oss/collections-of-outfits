OUTFIT: This post-apocalyptic Siberian outfit has a powerful, imposing silhouette built around extreme winter protection and rugged masculine luxury. The dominant garment is an enormous knee-length black-brown sheepskin coat with dense natural fur, broad shoulders, a massive raised collar, wide sleeves, and heavy leather reinforcement across the shoulders and elbows. Beneath it is a fitted charcoal thermal sweater with a high neck, creating contrast against the oversized coat. The lower body consists of heavy dark brown wool trousers tucked into massive black leather winter boots with thick insulated soles. A wide aged leather belt with large metal hardware and several functional pouches emphasizes the waist. Thick black leather gloves complete the look. COLOR PALETTE: black-brown, charcoal, dark leather, muted steel.
