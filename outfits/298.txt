This minimalist, casual streetwear outfit features a balanced dark green and solid black color palette. The upper body is defined by a tight, form-fitting dark green cropped sweater made of a thin, ribbed-knit fabric with strong stretch and recovery. It features a high mock neckline, tightly hugging long sleeves, and a straight-cropped hemline that exposes a slim strip of the midriff. The dominant lower layer consists of high-waisted, wide-leg black tailored trousers made from a smooth, structured woven fabric with sharp vertical front pleats and side slip pockets, draping loosely to the floor. The ensemble features no jewelry or wearable accessories. Completing the look is a pair of chunky, flat white platform athletic sneakers.
