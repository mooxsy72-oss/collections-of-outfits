This Victorian-inspired Gothic evening gown features a dramatic palette dominated by deep burgundy-wine velvet contrasted with antique cream floral lace and pearl accents. A structured, boned bodice with princess seams and a pointed V-shaped basque waist cinches tightly before flaring into a voluminous, gathered high-low skirt that sweeps the floor in back while cutting diagonally across the front to expose the lower legs. The square sweetheart neckline is bordered by gathered cream lace trim centered with an oval cameo brooch, leaving the neck, upper chest, and hands bare. Voluminous leg-of-mutton sleeves with puffed shoulders narrow at the forearms before flaring into double-tiered cream lace engageante ruffles. Matching cream lace ruffles border the asymmetrical skirt opening, paired with burgundy pointed-toe heels and a multi-strand pearl choker with a cameo drop.
