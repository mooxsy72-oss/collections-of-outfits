OUTFIT: This ensemble combines a more feminine silhouette with practical cold-weather dacha clothing. The main garment is a long loose-fitting charcoal wool coat reaching just below the knees, featuring a high wrap collar, wide sleeves, large patch pockets, and a heavy soft drape. Beneath it is a fitted cream thermal sweater and a dark olive knitted vest creating subtle layered texture. The lower body consists of thick black leggings layered beneath a knee-length dark brown canvas work skirt with deep side pockets and a straight practical cut. Tall matte-brown rubber boots with thick soles protect the legs from wet grass and mud. Black knitted gloves and a small leather waist pouch complete the outfit. The silhouette combines the elegant vertical line of the long coat with practical layered lower garments.
