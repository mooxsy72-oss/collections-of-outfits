This fantasy-themed evening gown features a dominant matte black tone paired with vibrant, iridescent blue tones and shimmering silver embellishments.
The main garment is a floor-length, asymmetric one-shoulder gown crafted from heavy, structured matte black crepe that tightly contours the hips and waist before pooling into a soft train. It leaves the left shoulder, collarbone, and right arm exposed. A dramatic, high-sweeping diagonal slit begins at the left hip, heavily exposing the left leg.
Framing the entire left side of the gown are two large, rigid, wing-shaped panels constructed to resemble butterfly wings. These structured pieces feature a gradient pattern shifting from deep cerulean blue at the edges to a bright sky blue near the center, accented by radiating pleated textures. The outer borders of the wings, along with the entire edge of the gown's high slit, are heavily encrusted with a dense, glittering trim of silver rhinestones, crystals, and metallic sequins. On the feet are matching pointed-toe pumps completely covered in fine silver glitter.
