This avant-garde, masquerade-themed evening gown features a monochromatic silver-white palette, combining heavily detailed lace textures with an exceptionally voluminous feather-adorned lower section, excluding any clutch.
The core garment is an ultra-fitted lace bodysuit featuring an integrated corset bodice, a high mock collar neckline, and semi-sheer long sleeves. The right arm is embellished with wrapping silver metallic ribbon tendrils. Attached to the aggressively cinched waist is an exaggerated, floor-sweeping open-front train skirt covered densely in thick layers of voluminous white ostrich feathers mixed with shimmering silver glitter panels, completely exposing the right leg and thigh. Crowning the look is a structured silver metallic masquerade mask decorated with ornate filigree cutout patterns. The hair is styled in a sleek, polished low chignon bun with a clean side part.
On the feet are silver metallic strappy sandals with high stiletto heels.
