OUTFIT: This classic dacha work outfit is built around a heavy chocolate-brown cotton canvas chore coat with a relaxed waist-length cut, broad flat collar, straight sleeves, three large patch pockets, and sturdy metal buttons. Underneath is a muted cream henley layered over a dark thermal shirt. The lower body consists of loose dark olive trousers made from heavy cotton twill, with a straight cut and reinforced knees. Mid-calf black rubber work boots with deep tread provide protection from wet soil. Dark brown knit gloves and a simple canvas apron complete the rugged rural silhouette.
