OUTFIT: fantasy-ballet or romantic goth-inspired mini dress relies on a rich burgundy-red palette accented with contrasting white details. The garment balances a rigid, elongated torso against an extremely voluminous, multi-layered skirt.The core garment is a strappy, corseted dress featuring a structured, elongated bodice that extends past the natural waist into a dramatic pointed Basque baseline. Made from heavy, high-shine burgundy satin with strong shape retention, it is reinforced by vertical boning channels highlighted with delicate white lace overlay strips. The straight-across neckline features a wide panel of horizontally draped satin across the chest. The dress is supported by thin satin shoulder straps that tie into dainty bows with long trailing ribbons at each shoulder, leaving the neck, collarbones, and arms completely exposed.The visually dominant skirt is gathered tightly at the low Basque waist, flaring out into an oversized, high-volume bubble silhouette that cuts across the mid-thigh. It is constructed from layers of gathered burgundy satin intermixed with sheer tulle paneling. The hem is lined with a delicate white scalloped lace trim. Both the bodice and the skirt layers are sporadically embellished with scattered, small white pearl beads that mimic morning dew.
