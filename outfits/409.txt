This high-contrast, minimalist outfit features a bicolored, solid design without patterns. The secondary upper piece is a sleek, tight-fitting black leather tube top constructed from mid-weight, semi-matte faux or genuine leather with a subtle sweetheart neckline, completely exposing the shoulders, arms, and chest. It is tucked cleanly into the dominant lower piece: a high-waisted, bright white wrap skort made of a crisp, structured woven fabric that retains its clean geometry. The skort features an architectural, sharp asymmetrical front flap overlay that extends into an angled hemline, leaving the legs completely bare. Accessories include minimalist neutral slip-on heeled mules with a clear plastic toe strap and a small, structured black suede shoulder bag accented by a gold chain strap and central gold hardware closure.
