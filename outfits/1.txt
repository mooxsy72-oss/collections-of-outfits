OUTFIT: floor-length dress and matching woven sash belong to a traditional Slavic folk clothing tradition, closely resembling a heavily embroidered vyshyvanka dress. The dominant color is an unbleached, off-white ivory, while the secondary color is a vibrant crimson red used for dense geometric embroidery.The dress is woven from a medium-weight, structured linen or hemp fabric that holds soft volume and drops in long, crisp vertical folds. It features a loose silhouette with full body coverage, completely concealing the neck, torso, arms, and legs. The construction includes a high, fitted mandarin collar and voluminous bishop sleeves gathered deeply at the shoulders and cuffs into narrow wristbands. Intricate, geometric cross-stitch style embroidery forms dense red horizontal bands across the upper sleeves, forearms, collar, and a vertical panel down the center chest. Smaller, isolated geometric star motifs are scattered across the mid-sleeves.The main accessory is a wide, stiffly woven textile sash wrapped around the natural waist line. It is decorated with repeating red geometric diamond patterns, with its long fringed tail hanging vertically past the hips to provide a strong focal point.
