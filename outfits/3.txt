This Central Asian and East Asian qipao-deel fusion winter ensemble features a palette dominated by deep espresso brown, warm tan fur, and cream shearling, providing full body coverage except for the hands. Dominating the silhouette is a tailored, ankle-length dark brown wool-crepe dress with a high cream-piped mandarin collar, frog closures, and a diagonal cross-chest lapel trimmed with plush tan fur and dangling silver-gray fringe. Wide, full-length sleeves end in oversized, thick cream curly-shearling cuffs. A beaded tassel dangles from the right-side frog buttons above a high, cream-piped side skirt slit revealing opaque, fleece-lined dark espresso-brown thermal tights beneath. A fluffy tan raccoon-fur coonskin cap with a striped tail and mid-calf tan fur mukluk boots with dark brown straps and fur pom-poms complete the look.
