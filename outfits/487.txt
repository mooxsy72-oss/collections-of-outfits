OUTFIT: This ensemble is designed for winter greenhouse and garden work, combining warmth with easy movement. The dominant layer is a cropped navy quilted jacket with a high collar, elastic cuffs, and water-resistant matte fabric. Beneath it is a thick grey fleece hoodie. The lower body consists of relaxed tan insulated work trousers with articulated knees and multiple utility pockets. Waterproof olive-green winter boots with deep tread protect against snow and mud. Dark fleece gloves and a compact cross-body canvas tool bag complete the outfit. The silhouette is practical, compact, and deliberately easy to move in.
