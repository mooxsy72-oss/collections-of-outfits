OUTFIT: outfit represents a contemporary minimalist, monochromatic autumn fashion aesthetic, emphasizing contrasting textures rather than prints. The silhouette features a stark, dramatic contrast in proportions: a highly fitted, streamlined upper body that flows into an exceptionally voluminous, sweeping lower half. The color palette is completely monochromatic, dominated by rich, deep espresso brown with slightly lighter warm brown tones in the suede fabric texture.The upper garment is a thin, form-fitting turtleneck sweater with a ribbed knit texture. It has a high, folded collar and extra-long, slim-fit sleeves that extend past the wrists, clinging tightly to the torso. The lower garment is a high-waisted, floor-length maxi skirt with an extremely wide, full-circle A-line flare. Crafted from a heavy, thick faux-suede or genuine suede fabric, the skirt features a flat, wide waistband and deep, structured folds that catch the light to show a subtle, mottled texture.Footwear consists of pointed-toe leather boots in dark espresso brown, featuring a slouched, gathered shaft that disappears under the skirt's hemline.Layered over the sweater are two delicate silver-toned pendant necklaces, one featuring a distinctive planetary orbit motif. A matching structured leather tote bag is held in the hand. The outfit provides total body coverage.
