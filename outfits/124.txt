This Caucasian traditional-inspired fantasy formal gown (resembling a chokha or arkhalig overdress) features a rich burgundy-crimson palette accented with metallic silver-white embroidery and metalwork, providing full body coverage except for the hands. Crafted from structured, medium-heavy matte burgundy wool-crepe, the tailored floor-length coat-dress features a high standing mandarin collar, sharply padded shoulders, slim wrist-length sleeves with pointed silver-embroidered cuffs, and dramatic, elongated cape sleeves hanging down past the knees. Nine horizontal silver-frogged toggle closures fasten the center chest bib, bordered by silver-white braided piping and floral vine embroidery along the shoulders, open center-front skirt slit, and sweeping A-line hem. An ornate, wide embossed silver filigree waist belt with a central medallion cinches the waist.
