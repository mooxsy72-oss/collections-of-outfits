This preppy, academic-inspired outfit features dark gray and light blue as its dominant colors, with solid fabrics and textures. The topmost layer is a chunky, thick dark gray cable-knit sweater vest with a relaxed silhouette, ribbed V-neck, and wide ribbed hem. Beneath it sits an oversized, lightweight light blue button-down shirt with long, voluminous billowy sleeves, structured cuffs, and a pointed collar. The shirt hem extends past the vest's waistline. A slim, smooth black necktie featuring a small silver triangular logo plate is tied under the collar. A structured, short dark gray knife-pleated miniskirt sits low on the hips. The lower thighs and knees are exposed. The look finishes with mid-calf black cotton socks and chunky, glossy black patent leather penny loafers with thick lug soles, paired with a black crescent-shaped leather shoulder bag adorned with silver studs and buckles.
