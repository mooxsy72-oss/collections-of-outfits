This fantasy historical costume draws inspiration from Baroque and Rococo aesthetics, combining a deep burgundy dominant color with white secondary accents and gold trims. The outfit features a fitted, structured corset-style bodice made of heavy burgundy velvet with a low scoop neckline, framed by wide, metallic gold lace trim. The bodice incorporates a contrast white V-shaped front panel with intricate gold embroidery. Three-quarter length bishop sleeves are voluminous, gathered at the shoulders, and end in wide, structured gold-trimmed cuffs embellished with a central faux gemstone. The lower body features a dramatically flared, short open overskirt in smooth, lustrous burgundy satin, embroidered with gold vines and red roses. This layer parts to reveal a voluminous, tiered white underskirt with fine lace ruffles. The ensemble leaves the upper chest, collarbones, and lower thighs exposed, paired with stark white, fitted thigh-high leather boots with pointed toes. The only accessory is a layered pearl choker with a ruby pendant.
