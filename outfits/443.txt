OUTFIT: This outfit presents a hardened post-apocalyptic commander with a clean, authoritative silhouette rather than excessive tactical equipment. The dominant garment is a long dark charcoal military-inspired wool coat reaching below the knees, constructed from extremely heavy fabric with broad shoulders, a high structured collar, double-breasted metal fasteners, and subtle black leather reinforcement along the cuffs. Beneath it is a fitted black turtleneck and dark tailored wool trousers. Heavy black leather boots, a wide belt with a steel buckle, leather gloves, and a single rugged shoulder harness complete the look. COLOR PALETTE: charcoal, black, gunmetal, dark brown.
