OUTFIT: warrior attire reflects an early medieval Slavic or Old Rus warrior tradition, characterized by a functional pairing of a textile undershirt, chainmail hauberk, loose trousers, and classic leather military accents. The silhouette is heavily structured, defined by the weight, stiffness, and metallic texture of the armor overlaying soft textile layers. The dominant color palette is metallic silver-gray and natural off-white, with dark blue and brown serving as secondary grounding tones.The base layer consists of a knee-length undershirt or tunic in coarse, mid-weight off-white linen or wool, featuring a simple crew neck and full-length sleeves. Layered directly over it is a thigh-length chainmail hauberk made of interlocking iron or steel rings. It has a loose, straight fit, short elbow-length sleeves, and a split hem at the center to allow mobility. The armor provides massive weight and sits rigidly over the shoulders and torso.The lower body is clad in loose, comfortable trousers of a deep dark-blue woven fabric, which drape softly and gather where they are tucked into tall leather boots. These brown boots are unstructured, calf-high, and exhibit a worn, matte texture.A thick, dark brown leather belt with a simple metallic buckle cinches the waist tightly over the chainmail, supporting a sheathed sword scabbard at the left hip. The outfit provides full body coverage below the neck.
