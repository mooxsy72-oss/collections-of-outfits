This Renaissance-peasant and dark fantasy-inspired gown features a muted palette dominated by slate charcoal-gray and weathered olive-gray, accented with antique gold embroidery. A structured, boned underbust corset waist cincher in heavy matte olive-gray fabric tapers to a pointed V-shaped basque hem, bordered by gold piping and densely embroidered with intricate antique gold floral scrollwork. Layered beneath it is a flowing, floor-length chemise gown in lightweight, softly draping matte charcoal-gray cotton-linen. It features an elasticized off-the-shoulder sweetheart neckline with delicate ruffle trim, leaving the shoulders, collarbones, upper chest, and hands bare. Exaggeratedly voluminous, full-length bishop sleeves billow out before gathering into distressed, frayed ruffle cuffs, while a heavily gathered, floor-sweeping A-line skirt falls below the waist. A layered dark tarnished metal chain necklace completes the look.
