This fantasy Russian folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant terracotta-orange and gold palette paired with stark white secondary accents. Providing full body coverage from the collarbones to the floor, it comprises a floor-length, flared sarafan (jumper dress) made of heavy, structured terracotta-orange brocade with a large, repeating woven gold floral damask pattern. The dress features thick shoulder straps, a square neckline bordered by gold metallic trim, and a decorative gold vertical band down the center front adorned with tiny dome buttons. Layered underneath is a crisp white linen under-shirt featuring an scoop neckline and oversized, voluminous bishop sleeves gathered at the wrists into tight white cuffs. The look includes dark brown, closed-toe leather shoes peeking from the hem. A long, multi-strand pearl necklace rests elegantly across the chest.
