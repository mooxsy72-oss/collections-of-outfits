OUTFIT: This outfit belongs to a solitary traveler crossing frozen Siberian wilderness, emphasizing extreme insulation and improvised protection. The dominant garment is a huge charcoal-grey padded parka reaching below the knees, made from thick weather-resistant canvas with a massive hood, high collar, and oversized utility pockets lined with dirty beige fur. Beneath it are several thick brown wool layers and a faded olive quilted vest. Extremely wide dark brown insulated trousers are stacked above heavy felt-lined winter boots. A battered canvas backpack with rolled blankets and rope ties sits high on the back. A large dark brown fur trapper hat and oversized mittens complete the look. COLOR PALETTE: charcoal, faded brown, dirty olive, beige, blackened metal.
