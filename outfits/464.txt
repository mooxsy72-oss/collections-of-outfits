Its straight sleeves terminate in dramatic, oversized red patent leather trumpet-style folded cuffs that match the high-gloss finish of the pointed-toe red kitten heels, which feature sharp metallic gold toe caps. A textured white wool beret and slim, black cat-eye sunglasses complete the look.
