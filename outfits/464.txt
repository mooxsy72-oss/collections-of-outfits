OUTFIT: avant-garde streetwear ensemble features an eclectic, multi-layered color blocking scheme dominated by matte black and heather gray, offering total body coverage.The primary layer consists of a tight, secondary-skin top in thin, high-stretch jersey, tucked into very short, structured heather-gray wool shorts with a curved, high-rise waistband. The dominant outer layer is a heavy, ankle-length duster coat in thick black wool felt with structured, padded shoulders and massive peak lapels. On the legs are opaque black tights providing full coverage under the garments. Footwear consists of structured, knee-high black leather boots with a smooth matte texture that provides a sleek, polished finish to the long, column-like lower silhouette.
