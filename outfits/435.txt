The lower body consists of oversized charcoal cargo trousers with reinforced knees and thick fabric stacking above massive black leather combat boots. Heavy leather gloves, a wide utility belt, and a rugged dark canvas shoulder bag complete the outfit. COLOR PALETTE: charcoal, black, dark brown, steel grey, dirty fur.
