This traditional Russian folk ensemble features a distinctive crescent-shaped kokoshnik headdress, richly embellished with gold brocade embroidery and hanging pearl strands that frame the face. The ankle-length outfit consists of a voluminous white linen rubakha shirt with oversized, billowy bishop sleeves gathered at the lace-trimmed cuffs, layered underneath a structured, heavy sarafan dress. The sarafan is rendered in a dark gold and black floral-patterned brocade, featuring a high-waisted bodice and a wide, flowing skirt. A stiff, cream-colored linen apron overlay dominates the front, detailed with dense red geometric embroidery bands and a pointed white lace hem. A long, woven red and gold sash wraps around the waist, knotted at the hip with long dangling tassels. On the feet are woven bast lapti shoes paired with thick white linen leg wraps cross-tied up to the shins, ensuring complete body coverage.
