The elegant evening outfit features an all-black formal maxi dress combining structured, form-fitting elements with a fluid, cascading lower half. The dress is crafted from a smooth, mid-weight synthetic jersey fabric with high stretch and shape retention. An integrated, narrow fabric band forms a delicate, thin mock-neck choker collar around the neck, creating a distinct, wide horizontal crescent cutout that completely exposes the collarbones, upper chest, and tops of the shoulders.The closely cinched waist flows into a fluid, subtly gathered A-line maxi skirt that flares out slightly and drapes softly down to the ankles. Footwear consists of classic, pointed-toe black leather stiletto pumps with thin high heels. Handheld accessories include a structured, compact black leather handbag with a clean rectangular shape and a short, rigid top handle. A delicate silver pendant necklace resting in the center of the chest cutout serves as the sole wearable jewelry piece.
