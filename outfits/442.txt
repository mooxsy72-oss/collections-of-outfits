OUTFIT: This post-apocalyptic mechanic outfit combines industrial workwear with severe winter layering and a powerful masculine silhouette. The dominant garment is a huge dark rust-brown insulated work jacket made from thick canvas, with broad shoulders, a heavy shearling collar, oversized chest pockets, reinforced elbows, and worn brass hardware. Beneath it is a black ribbed thermal sweater. The lower body consists of loose charcoal work trousers with leather knee patches, thick suspenders, and heavy black leather boots with reinforced toes. A wide tool belt, leather gloves, metal carabiners, and a rugged canvas shoulder strap add functional detail without making the outfit look overly tactical. COLOR PALETTE: rust brown, charcoal, black, dark leather, aged brass.
