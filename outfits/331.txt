This mid-century-inspired, mid-tone brown ensemble features an ultra-cinched New Look silhouette dominated by a structured midi sheath dress. The sleeveless dress is made of stiff matte fabric with strong shape retention, featuring a high mock neck, an extremely tapered waist, and a structured bow at the back belt line. A prominent, matching brown silk headscarf is wrapped closely around the head and neck, with dramatic, voluminous ends fluttering backward. Accessories include matching opera-length gathered brown fabric gloves, thick black-framed cat-eye sunglasses, and a structured cylindrical vanity case bag in a tan monogram print. On the feet are bi-color stiletto pumps with pointed white toes and black counters.
