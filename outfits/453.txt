OUTFIT: This two-piece sleepwear ensemble blends a dark, gothic aesthetic with casual comfort, utilizing a monochromatic palette of solid jet-black paired with a high-contrast black and white plaid pattern. The tightly cropped top juxtaposed against long trousers leaves the entire midriff, arms, shoulders, upper chest, and neck fully exposed.The top is a form-fitting, underwired bustier-style camisole crafted from a medium-weight, plush black velvet or velour with high shape retention. It features thin shoulder straps, a sweetheart neckline, and scalloped edges lined with delicate black floral lace trims along the top cups and bottom band. A small, metallic silver bat-shaped charm rests at the center gore as a thematic focal point. The secondary garment consists of loose, straight-leg pajama pants featuring a thick elasticated waistband. The fabric is a soft, mid-weight brushed cotton flannel or fleece that hangs with a relaxed, straight drape down to the ankles.
