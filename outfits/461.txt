OUTFIT: modern streetwear ensemble relies on contrasting textures and a high-contrast palette of dominant black and cream-beige, offering full body coverage without exposed skin.The upper half features an intentionally oversized, voluminous cropped puffer jacket in matte black nylon. It is defined by thick horizontal quilting, a high stand-up padded collar, and bulky sleeves. The jacket's high loft creates structural, shape-retaining volume that dramatically widens the upper silhouette. Layered beneath is a thick, cream-beige sweater with a dense ribbed-knit mock neckline. The lower half consists of ultra-tight, second-skin leggings or trousers in a matching cream-beige tone. They feature a fine vertical ribbed texture, heavy stretch, and an elasticated high waist that hugs the body closely.The major accessory is a chunky, cream-beige beanie knitted from thick yarn, featuring distinct, pointy cat-ear protrusions at the crown. An ochre-yellow plush backpack with narrow adjustable straps is worn over the shoulders.
