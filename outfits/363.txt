This modern evening ensemble features a monochromatic color distribution rendered entirely in a solid jet-black palette, accented by a classic cream-white accessory.
The primary piece is a floor-length, column-silhouette gown combining structured and fluid elements. The upper section is a mock-layered bodice made of smooth, mid-weight black jersey fabric, featuring a wide, square-cut scoop neckline and long, slim sleeves providing full arm coverage. The center midriff section is constructed as an integrated corset panel made of sheer, unlined black floral lace with visible vertical boning seams. This connects to a high-waisted, form-fitting maxi skirt that hugs the hips tightly before draping down into a fluid column hemline.
Footwear consists of matching black leather pumps with a sharp pointed toe line and a low kitten heel. A classic, short single-strand cream pearl necklace completes the accessories.
