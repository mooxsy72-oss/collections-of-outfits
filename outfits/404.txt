OUTFIT: rock-glam ensemble features a dark, high-contrast palette of washed grey denim, sheer black lace, and polished black leather. The loose-fitting, oversized denim jacket dominates the upper body with its thick, rigid structure, featuring a distressed acid-wash finish and large white collegiate-style lettering across the chest. Beneath it sits a form-fitting, semi-sheer black lace camisole top with thin spaghetti straps, a scalloped sweetheart neckline, and underwire cups, revealing the upper chest and collarbones.The lower body is defined by an ultra-short, distressed denim mini skirt layered over a sheer black lace extension panel with side lace-up ties, exposing the midriff and thighs. The skirt has traditional belt loops holding a thin black leather belt with silver hardware, complemented by a draped silver pocket chain.Visual volume balances the bulky, wide-sleeved jacket against the skin-tight, abbreviated inner layers. Tall, over-the-knee black leather boots feature heavy slouching textures, high stiletto heels, and pointed toes. Accessories include a structured black leather trapeze handbag with a silver lock closure, layered silver chain necklaces with prominent gothic cross pendants, and matching dangling cross earrings, solidifying the edgy, layered aesthetic.
