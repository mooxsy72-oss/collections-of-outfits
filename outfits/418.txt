OUTFIT:glamorous evening ensemble leans into a high-fashion aesthetic, contrasting a heavy, structured outerwear piece with a minimalist, close-fitting base. The dominant garment is a knee-length, loose-fitting mink fur coat in a rich, multi-tonal honey-brown shade. It features wide, horizontal panel stitching across the body, a substantial notched lapel collar, and loose, straight-cut sleeves. The fur is thick, low-pile, and plush, providing soft structural volume that creates a heavy silhouette.Beneath the open coat, a tight, low-cut black satin slip dress fits closely to the torso, ending at the mid-thigh to expose the legs. The neckline features thin spaghetti straps and delicate black lace trim detailing along the chest.The lower legs are covered in sheer, ultra-fine black hosiery. Footwear consists of black leather high-heeled sandals accented by thin, multi-strand ankle straps. A multi-layered, delicate white pearl strand necklace serves as the primary jewelry accessory.
