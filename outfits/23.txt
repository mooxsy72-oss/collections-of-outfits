Traditional Russian royal ensemble consists of a compact, rigid kokoshnik and a floor-length, A-line velvet caftan-gown with concealed pointed-toe black velvet shoes. The outfit features a dominant deep black and metallic gold color distribution with white pearl accents. The gown provides complete body coverage with a high mandarin collar and voluminous bishop sleeves gathered into rigid, elongated cuffs. It is constructed from heavy, dense black velvet that retains structure and forms deep, vertical drapes. Symmetrical, medium-scale gold embroidery with fleur-de-lis and floral motifs heavily embellishes the collar, front closure, cuffs, and lower skirt. The primary visual focal point is the scaled-down, solid gold-brocade kokoshnik headdress with a non-porous, rigid surface completely covered in dense gold embroidery, inset dark gems, a pearl forehead net, and hanging pearl strands, tightly framing the head.
