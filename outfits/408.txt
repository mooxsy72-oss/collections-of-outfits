OUTFIT: modern Japanese "kawaii" and "jirai kei" influenced outfit features a dominant pastel pink and soft white palette, heavily relying on extreme, oversized proportions to create visual volume.The primary layer is an intentionally oversized, mid-thigh length chunky rib-knit cardigan in pastel pink. It features dramatic drop shoulders, highly voluminous lantern sleeves that cover the hands, two front patch pockets, and large round buttons securing the deep V-neckline. Peeking from underneath is a structured white cotton button-up shirt with a pointed collar, accented by a contrasting satin-textured pink bow tie. A hint of a short, pleated pink skirt hem extends below the cardigan.The lower body features full skin coverage, beginning with sheer off-white tights adorned with a vertical open-work diamond lattice pattern down the side, punctuated by tiny pink ribbon bows. Layered over the tights are exaggeratedly thick, slouchy white knit leg warmers that puddle over the footwear. The shoes are pink platform mary janes with thick white rubber soles and a decorative white ruffle trim running across the wide instep straps.
