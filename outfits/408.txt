OUTFIT: fantasy-inspired cocktail dress mimics the anatomy of an inverted flower, pulling from fairy core and whimsical couture traditions. The color scheme is dominated by pastel pink, accented with secondary iridescent silver, lavender, and soft white details.The garment is a strapless, structural mini dress featuring a fitted, corseted bodice with a sweetheart neckline. The bodice is constructed from a heavy pink satin base fabric, entirely overlaid with layers of sheer organza. It is densely embellished with three-dimensional floral appliques, iridescent sequins, and metallic embroidery that clusters along the upper neckline and tapers down toward the waist.The visually dominant skirt is engineered to resemble large, overlapping flower petals. These oversized petals are stiffened with internal wiring or structural horsehair braid trims along their curved edges to maintain an extreme, ballooned volume that completely covers the hips and upper thighs. The skirt is layered in two tiers of petals, covered with delicate, scattered purple and pink sequined floral details. Dangling beneath the hem and from the bodice are multiple long, delicate beaded clear strings adorned with tiny white fabric flowers and butterfly embellishments. A delicate, single-strand silver rhinestone collar necklace finishes the look.
