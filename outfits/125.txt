This Caucasian traditional-inspired fantasy formal gown (resembling a chokha or arkhalig overdress) features a deep emerald-green palette accented with metallic antique-gold embroidery and metalwork, providing full body coverage except for the hands. Crafted from structured, medium-heavy matte emerald-green wool-crepe, the tailored floor-length coat-dress features a high standing mandarin collar, sharply padded shoulders, slim wrist-length sleeves with pointed gold-embroidered cuffs, and dramatic, elongated cape sleeves hanging down past the knees. Nine horizontal gold-frogged toggle closures fasten the center chest bib, bordered by gold braided piping and floral vine embroidery along the shoulders, open center-front skirt slit, and sweeping A-line hem. An ornate, wide embossed gold filigree waist belt with a central medallion cinches the waist.
