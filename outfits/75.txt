This traditional Slavic chemise provides minimal body coverage, leaving the shoulders, collarbones, arms, lower legs, and feet entirely bare and barefoot. The single-piece garment is a thigh-length shift with a relaxed, billowy silhouette that flows loosely without a defined waist. Crafted from a lightweight, semi-sheer white cotton or linen cambric textile, the material exhibits a soft, rumpled texture with a fluid drape, pooling into soft folds around the lap.
