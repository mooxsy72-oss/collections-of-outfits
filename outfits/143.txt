This traditional Russian folk costume offers complete coverage, pairing a white rubakha (chemise) with a dominant burgundy sarafan (pinafore dress). The mid-weight rubakha features a gathered neckline and voluminous, billowy balloon sleeves tapering into tight cuffs. Layered over it, the floor-length, heavy-weight sarafan creates a stiff A-line drape, decorated with a vertical gold-embroidered geometric trim down the front center and a matching thick band along the wide hem. Beneath the hem, simple brown leather shoes peek out.The hair is styled into two thick, tightly woven braids starting behind the ears, extending over the shoulders down to the waist. Red satin ribbons are interwoven through each braid, finishing with dangling, trailing ends. A curved, rigid tiara-style headband covered in rows of tiny white pearls sits securely across the crown of the head.
