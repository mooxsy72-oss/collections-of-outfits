This fantasy historical gown draws heavily from Victorian and Rococo aesthetics, featuring a monochrome, dominant ivory-white color palette rich in mixed textures. The upper garment consists of a tightly fitted, structured corset bodice with clean vertical boning channels, a plunging sweetheart neckline, a pointed dropped waist, and a subtle damask floral texture. It features a dramatic off-the-shoulder neckline adorned with two rows of wide, tiered ruffles lined with fine lace trim. The voluminous, long bishop sleeves are gathered tightly at the upper arm, puff out widely in smooth fabric, and finish with flared cuffs decorated with intricate lace panels. The floor-length, sweeping skirt is constructed from layered, asymmetric tiers combining sharply pressed plissé pleats and detailed floral lace panels, generating immense structural volume. The outfit leaves the shoulders, collarbones, and upper chest fully exposed.
