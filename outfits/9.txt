This Arctic indigenous and Central Asian nomadic-inspired winter fantasy ensemble features a palette dominated by warm cream-beige, snowy white fur, and muted taupe-gray, accented with terracotta red, charcoal, and turquoise, providing complete body coverage except for the hands. Dominating the silhouette is an oversized, calf-length asymmetrical wrap coat (deel / parka) in heavy matte cream-beige wool-felt with an exaggeratedly thick, plush white fox-fur shawl collar, white fur-trimmed diagonal lapel, and wide patterned cuffs. Woven bands of circular solar medallions and geometric borders in terracotta, charcoal, and white decorate the cuffs, lower coat hem, and dangling hem tabs, worn over a pleated taupe-gray ankle-length underskirt. An embroidered charcoal-and-terracotta sash belt suspends a beaded amulet with white fur pom-poms, red tassels, and two small embroidered leather hip pouches, paired with layered bead necklaces, embroidered headbands, and fur-trimmed winter boots.
