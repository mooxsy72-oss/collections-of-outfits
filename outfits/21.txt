This East Asian hanfu-inspired winter fantasy ensemble features a palette dominated by matte charcoal-navy wool, deep crimson, and plush cream shearling fur, accented with slate blue and white embroidery, providing full body coverage except for the hands. Underneath sits a fitted crimson tunic with a high frog-fastened mandarin collar and a circular white beast medallion embroidered at the chest. Dominating the silhouette is a calf-length, asymmetrical cross-collar charcoal-navy overcoat (ao / changao) edged with thick cream fur along its collar, diagonal lapel, wide flared sleeve cuffs, upper-arm seams, and sweeping hem. Embroidered white-and-blue cloud-wave borders decorate the lower hem and cuffs, with matching circular medallions on the sleeves. A wide charcoal-and-crimson waist sash with hanging blue-striped ribbons, cream tassels, a pleated crimson side-drape, baggy charcoal trousers, and tall black lace-up boots complete the look.
