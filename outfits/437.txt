OUTFIT: This post-apocalyptic outfit is designed for a powerful lone traveler crossing frozen Siberian wastelands, combining primitive materials with a refined masculine silhouette. The dominant garment is a massive mid-calf coat made from thick dark charcoal wool and brown sheepskin, with a structured oversized shape, broad shoulders, high collar, and wide sleeves. Beneath it is a fitted black ribbed sweater and a dark leather vest. The lower body consists of loose black wool trousers tucked into heavy dark brown leather boots with thick soles and metal reinforcement. A broad leather harness crosses the torso, carrying small utility pouches and a metal flask. Thick weathered leather gloves complete the outfit. COLOR PALETTE: charcoal, black, dark brown, muted steel.
