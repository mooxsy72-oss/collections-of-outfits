OUTFIT: ensemble represents a historical European early medieval peasant or agrarian worker costume, defined by its utilitarian tunic, protective canvas apron, leg wraps, and functional working tools. The silhouette features moderate, organic volume from gathered textiles and layered components, cinched firmly at the middle. The color distribution relies entirely on muted, earthy tones, with moss green and muddy brown dominating the torso, balanced by light beige and dark brown on the lower legs.The base layer is a knee-length tunic of a relaxed fit, featuring a scoop neckline with a buttoned Henley-style placket and loose sleeves pushed up to the forearms. The fabric is a mid-weight, heavily textured slub linen or coarse wool that drapes softly. Over the tunic sits a heavy work apron made of stiff, stained brown canvas with a frayed hem, secured by leather shoulder straps.Loose-fitting trousers in dark brown wool bunch over the knees before being tightly bound at the calves by criss-crossing cord ties over light beige linen leg wraps (winingas). On the feet are sturdy, low-profile leather ankle boots with a wrinkled, matte texture.A thick, braided hemp rope belt wraps twice around the waist over the apron, anchoring a small leather belt pouch with a flap closure on the left hip. The outfit provides full body coverage except for the lower arms and neck.
