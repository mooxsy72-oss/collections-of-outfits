OUTFIT: This rustic autumn dacha ensemble draws from classic Eastern European garden workwear while keeping the proportions clean and feminine. The dominant garment is a faded dark blue cotton chore jacket with a relaxed hip-length silhouette, rounded collar, large front patch pockets, sturdy metal buttons, and slightly rolled sleeves. Beneath it is a thick cream-and-brown checked flannel shirt layered over a thin thermal undershirt. The lower body consists of high-waisted dark green straight-leg trousers made from heavy cotton twill, with a loose fit through the thighs and reinforced knees. The footwear consists of worn dark brown leather lace-up boots with thick rubber soles and rounded toes. A long beige canvas gardening apron with several large pockets is tied over the trousers, while simple brown leather work gloves complete the outfit. The silhouette is relaxed, sturdy, layered, and intentionally practical, with natural autumn colors and visibly tactile fabrics throughout.
