OUTFIT: floor-length robe-like gown draws inspiration from traditional Middle Eastern, North African, or Byzantine caftans, defined by its column silhouette and long bell sleeves. The color palette is dominated by an unbleached, ivory-white hue with metallic gold embroidery and an array of contrasting colorful stacked necklaces.The main garment is a loose, straight-cut, floor-length caftan crafted from a fluid, lightweight linen or silk blend that exhibits soft drape, low stiffness, and subtle vertical fabric texture. It features an open scoop neckline framing the neck and upper chest, dropping into a long, continuous column silhouette with no defined waist. The long sleeves are fitted at the upper arm before flaring dramatically into wide, voluminous bell cuffs at the forearms. Delicate, linear gold embroidery borders the neckline, runs vertically down the center front seam, and trims the cuffs and shoulder seams.The primary accessories are layered, stacked statement necklaces of varied lengths that serve as a strong visual focal point against the open neckline. The arrangement includes a close-fitting white pearl choker, a thick strand of asymmetrical coral-red branch beads, a multi-strand metallic silver chain necklace, and a long, sweeping strand of small white pearls.
