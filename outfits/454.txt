OUTFIT: two-piece pajama set features a delicate, romantic aesthetic in a cream-white dominant color with a small-scale, repeating pattern of tan-brown hearts distributed evenly across both pieces. The tight-fitting ensemble leaves the arms, shoulders, collarbones, upper chest, and thighs fully exposed.The top is a form-fitting camisole with thin, non-adjustable spaghetti straps. It features a scoop neckline accented by a delicate white lace trim and a central vertical decorative placket with three small white buttons. The matching shorts are high-rise and ultra-short, featuring an elasticated waistband with a functional cream drawstring tied at the center. The leg openings are finished with a ruffled, lettuce-edge hemline.Both garments are constructed from a lightweight, soft, pointelle ribbed knit fabric with low thickness, a fluid drape, and high stretch. Wearable accessories consist of thick, chunky metallic bangles in polished gold and silver finishes stacked on both wrists.
