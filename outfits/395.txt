This military-inspired streetwear outfit features a balanced color distribution dominated by a green-based camouflage pattern, neutral medium gray, and matte black.The top layer is a structured, standard-fit field jacket made from durable, mid-weight cotton twill printed with a Flecktarn camouflage pattern consisting of small, dense spots in olive, dark green, brown, and black. It features a shirt-style collar, buttoned chest patch pockets, and a small German flag patch on the upper right sleeve. Beneath it sits a thick, relaxed-fit medium gray fleece pullover hoodie with a substantial double-layered hood framing the neck, long sleeves finishing in ribbed cuffs, and a kangaroo pocket.The bottom half consists of slim-fit black tactical cargo pants made of structured, lightweight cotton ripstop, featuring prominent rectangular utility pockets on the outer thighs. On the feet are classic high-top canvas sneakers in solid black with contrasting stark white rubber toe caps, white laces, and white soles.
