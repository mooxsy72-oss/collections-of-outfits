OUTFIT: This rugged autumn look centers on a heavy dark-brown suede work jacket with a relaxed waist-length silhouette, broad shoulders, thick cream shearling lining, a wide shearling collar, and sturdy leather cuffs. Beneath it is a dark charcoal ribbed sweater with a high neckline and substantial texture. The lower body consists of loose faded-black denim jeans with a straight leg and rigid drape. Thick brown leather work boots with reinforced toes and lugged soles anchor the outfit. Dark leather gloves and a wide aged-leather belt complete the warm, masculine silhouette.
