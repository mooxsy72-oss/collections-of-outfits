OUTFIT: This cold-weather gardening ensemble combines warm natural textiles with practical workwear. The main layer is a thick sleeveless oatmeal wool vest with a slightly oversized silhouette, broad shoulders, a high zip-up collar, and deep vertical pockets. Underneath is a dark forest-green flannel shirt with a relaxed fit and rolled cuffs. The lower body consists of high-waisted wide-leg brown corduroy trousers with pronounced vertical ridges and a heavy structured drape. On the feet are ankle-high dark green rubber garden boots with thick soles and rounded toes. A pair of brown leather gardening gloves and a simple canvas waist pouch provide functional finishing details. The silhouette is relaxed and layered, with the textured wool vest creating the main visual volume.
