OUTFIT: garment is an monochromatic, deep burgundy formal ball gown featuring a structured, form-fitting corset bodice and an intentionally oversized, voluminous floor-length skirt with an extended cathedral train.The bodice features a sweetheart neckline, exposed collarbones, chest, and upper back, with short, unstructured off-the-shoulder cap sleeves. The structured bodice tightly cinches at the natural waist, establishing a stark contrast with the dramatically gathered skirt. Underneath the skirt, a rigid structural framework or multi-layered petticoat holds its extreme, expansive shape retention and weight.The entire outfit is constructed from a heavy, multi-layered mesh tulle fabric overlaid with dense, tonal lace embroidery. This lace pattern covers the bodice and cascades down the front and hem of the skirt in organic, vine-like branches and botanical motifs. The texture is heavily tactile, embellished with thousands of metallic burgundy sequins and microscopic glass beads that create complex shimmering light reflection. The thick, structured drapery pools heavily on the floor, dominating the silhouette as the single primary garment.
