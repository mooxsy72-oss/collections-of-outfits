This fantasy Renaissance-inspired formal gown features a palette of warm ivory-cream accented with antique metallic gold embroidery. A structured, boned underbust corset bodice crafted from heavy cream brocade cinches sharply at the waist with a pointed V-shaped basque hem, densely embroidered with intricate gold scrollwork and bordered by gold piping. Above it sits a gathered cream silk sweetheart bustier that leaves the shoulders, collarbones, and upper chest bare. Detached, off-the-shoulder sheer ivory chiffon bishop sleeves feature puffed upper caps, gold-embroidered bicep bands, voluminous blouson forearms, wide gold-embroidered cuffs, and trailing floor-length chiffon streamers. A voluminous, pleated ivory chiffon A-line skirt falls to the floor, framing a flat central front panel richly embroidered with a vertical gold floral vine motif.
