This fantasy Russian folk-inspired ensemble features a dominant ice-blue and silver palette paired with stark white secondary accents. Providing full body coverage, the floor-length, A-line opashon coat is made of structured ice-blue damask fabric woven with large silver floral brocade patterns, featuring an embroidered central silver border band. It has long, slit cape-like hanging sleeves, revealing the voluminous, gathered white satin sleeves of an under-shirt tucked into tight silver-embroidered cuffs. A massive, stiff crescent-shaped kokoshnik headdress in matching ice-blue and silver brocade dominates the silhouette, heavily encrusted with pearls, silver filigree, and a draped bead fringe. Plush white faux-fur trims the high collar, coat hem, and matching hand muff. Closed-toe white leather shoes are concealed beneath the sweeping hemline.
