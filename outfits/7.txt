OUTFIT: fantasy outfit draws inspiration from traditional Slavic folk dress, resembling a multi-layered sarafan ensemble. The silhouette is dominated by an A-line, floor-length gown with high visual volume created by gathered fabric and wide, billowing sleeves.The inner layer is an off-white, lightweight, fluidly draping linen blouse. It features a gathered scoop neckline exposing the collarbones, and voluminous lantern sleeves gathered tightly at the wrists.The outer layer is a heavy, structured, slate-blue pinafore dress with a square neckline and wide shoulder straps. The fitted bodice is heavily embellished with dense, symmetrical floral embroidery in gold, orange, and red thread. Gold decorative trim borders the neckline and armholes.A prominent, wide rust-red satin ribbon acts as a sash, tied into a large bow at the high waist, with long tails draping down the center front of the unpatterned, flowing blue skirt.
