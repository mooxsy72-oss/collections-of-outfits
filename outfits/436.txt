OUTFIT: This rugged Siberian survival outfit combines expedition clothing with heavy industrial workwear, emphasizing strength, utility, and extreme cold protection. The dominant garment is an oversized dark olive waxed-canvas parka with a rigid high collar, broad shoulders, reinforced leather shoulder panels, oversized chest pockets, and thick beige-grey fur lining around the collar. Beneath it is a heavy charcoal wool sweater layered over a fitted thermal shirt. The lower body consists of extremely wide dark brown insulated trousers with reinforced knees and heavy leather straps, tucked into tall dark brown leather winter boots with thick rugged soles. A large weathered leather belt, heavy gloves, and a compact canvas utility pack complete the look. COLOR PALETTE: dark olive, charcoal, dark brown, grey fur, black leather.
