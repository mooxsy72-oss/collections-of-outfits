This academia-inspired look features a cropped brown plaid skirt suit paired with classic black high heels. The outfit relies heavily on a classic Glen plaid pattern in shades of brown, beige, and black. A structured, ultra-cropped blazer features sharp padded shoulders, prominent notched lapels, and long slim sleeves with visible white cuffs. Underneath, a crisp white collared dress shirt is cropped high, and paired with a wide, matte black necktie, leaving the entire midriff fully exposed. A matching low-rise plaid mini skirt sits on the hips with a structured, rigid drape. The legs are covered in sheer black tights. A quilted black leather handbag with a silver chain shoulder strap hangs from the hand. Sleek, pointed-toe black leather high-heeled pumps anchor the lower half, reinforcing a polished and sharp silhouette.
