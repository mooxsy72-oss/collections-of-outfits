OUTFIT: This winter orchard-working ensemble features a deep burgundy insulated field jacket with a slightly fitted waist, oversized sleeves, a high collar, and durable water-resistant fabric. Beneath it is a thick oatmeal fleece pullover. The lower body consists of loose dark charcoal work trousers with reinforced knees and ankle straps preventing snow from entering the boots. Tall dark olive rubber winter boots with insulated lining provide protection from wet snow. Thick grey mittens and a rugged canvas shoulder bag complete the look. The silhouette is functional, layered, and designed for movement between snow-covered trees.
