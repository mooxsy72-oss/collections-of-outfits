OUTFIT: contemporary look blends high-fashion luxury with traditional Russian folk aesthetics, characterized by a contrast between immense cold-weather volume and a streamlined base.The dominant garment is an oversized, ankle-length coat crafted from thick, long-pile silver fox or raccoon fur, displaying a variegated, multi-tonal brown, grey, and cream coloration. It features a massive, unstructured collar and heavily draped, voluminous sleeves that dominate the overall silhouette. Beneath the open coat sits a simple, body-conforming black mini dress or romper, leaving the legs exposed through thin, sheer black hosiery.Framing the face is a traditional Russian posadsky style headscarf, worn wrapped over the head and neck. The scarf is made from a lightweight, woven red wool fabric embellished with dense, multicolored floral paisley patterns and finished with a long, flowing red fringe. Footwear consists of structured, mid-calf black leather combat boots featuring a matte finish and a thick, chunky lugged platform sole.
