This fantasy Russian folk-inspired ensemble features a monochromatic, dominant silver-blue color palette. The floor-length, form-fitting mermaid gown is crafted from heavy, lustrous silver-blue satin that pools into a long sweeping train, providing full body coverage from the high mock collar down to the floor. Elaborate, raised white floral lace embroidery runs down the center front, over the hips, and highlights the hemline. The long sleeves feature gathered, puffed shoulders that taper into tight, matching embroidered cuffs. The silhouette is heavily dominated by a traditional kokoshnik headdress, intricately detailed with dense rows of faux pearls, geometric silver beadwork, a scalloped border, and a cascading fringe of pearl strands that frame the face. The look is completed by matching silver-blue satin pointed-toe pumps adorned with delicate white lace overlays that echo the gown's embroidery.
