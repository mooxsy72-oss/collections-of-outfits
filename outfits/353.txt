This Victorian Gothic tailored gown features a monochromatic slate-charcoal gray and matte black palette with complete full-body coverage. A tightly fitted, structured basque bodice crafted from heavy slate-gray damask jacquard woven with dense dark charcoal floral scrollwork cinches sharply at the waist into pointed front peplum tabs, fastened down the center with ornate silver-black filigree clasps. Its sweetheart bustline meets a high-collared sheer black floral lace yoke, paired with leg-of-mutton sleeves featuring puffed shoulders and slim, damask-patterned forearms. Dominating the lower silhouette is a floor-length slate-gray bustle skirt layered with a heavily draped, horizontally ruched overskirt apron bordered by black floral embroidery, over a pleated underskirt with matching scrollwork embroidery and a ruffled hem. Fitted dark gray gloves and black pointed-toe shoes complete the look.
