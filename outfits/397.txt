OUTFIT: monochromatic white mini dress blends modern fairy-fantasy aesthetics with corsetry design elements.The inner structure features a fitted corset bodice with opaque, molded underwire cups forming a sweetheart neckline. The main midriff panel is constructed from a sheer mesh or organza fabric, reinforced with vertical, opaque white structural boning strips that tapers down to a sharp V-shaped drop waist.Emerging from underneath the corset's back are sheer, lightweight bishop sleeves with wide, loose proportions. The fabric drapes softly down the arms, gathering tightly near the wrists with simple elastic or fabric ties to form dramatic, flared bell cuffs.The A-line skirt is heavily gathered at the low V-waistline, utilizing a semi-sheer, fluid chiffon fabric that flows loosely away from the body with lightweight movement, ending at a short mid-thigh length. The construction exposes the collarbones, neck, chest, and lower thighs.Layered loosely over the shoulders is a thick, structured midi-length trench coat in a matte, solid slate grey. It features an open front, a folded collar, and buttoned cuff straps on the sleeves, draping heavily down past the dress's hemline to create a stark textural and volume contrast.
