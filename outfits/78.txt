This multi-layered, casual ensemble features a dominant lavender maxi skirt with a dense, small-scale blue floral print, crafted from lightweight, fluid rayon or viscose that drapes softly down the legs. The skirt is high-waisted and features a dramatic, high thigh-high slit exposing the right leg. Tucked inside is a fitted, semi-sheer cream lace top with short sleeves and a scalloped sweetheart neckline. Layered loosely over the shoulders is an oversized, chunky cornflower blue knit cardigan featuring wide sleeves, dropped shoulders, and large matching buttons. The sole accessory is a delicate, close-fitting beaded choker necklace alternating red and white beads in a repeating floral motif.
