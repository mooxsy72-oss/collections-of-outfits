This fantasy fairy-inspired outfit features a monochrome, uniform stark white color palette defined by delicate openwork textures and iridescent structural elements. The dress features a structured, fitted corset-style bodice with clean vertical boning channels, a scoop neckline supported by thin spaghetti straps, and a small horizontal cutout right beneath the bust. The fabric is a lightweight white broderie anglaise or eyelet cotton with tiny, repeating geometric perforations. It terminates in a dropped V-waistline, opening into a short, mid-thigh length handkerchief miniskirt made of layered, asymmetric tiers that drape into sharp, pointed panels. Attached at the back are large, prominent, translucent white fairy wings with a highly detailed, iridescent silver veining structure that dominates the upper silhouette. The garment leaves the arms, shoulders, collarbones, and lower thighs fully exposed, paired with classic white pointed-toe patent leather pumps.
