OUTFIT:winter ensemble represents traditional Russian folk styling, defined by full upper-body coverage, rich dark tones, and intense natural textures.The dominant garment is a voluminous, full-length shuba style coat made from thick, dense dark brown and grey faux fur. It features an oversized silhouette, a high plush neckline, and wide, loose-fitting sleeves with prominent fluffy cuffs that create significant visual bulk around the arms.Layered over the head and shoulders is a traditional Pavlovo Posad style wool shawl, serving as the secondary focal piece. The shawl is crafted from lightweight, fluidly draping black woven wool finished with a fine knotted fringe. It features a dense, large-scale floral pattern composed of vibrant red and pink roses accented by green leaves and intricate golden-yellow paisley filigree borders. Underneath the shawl, a simple, non-distracting black fabric top covers the central chest area.
