OUTFIT: This outfit combines primitive craftsmanship with brutal winter survival clothing, giving the character a massive, physically imposing silhouette. The dominant garment is a sleeveless dark brown shearling coat layered over a huge charcoal wool sweater with thick ribbing and a high neck. The outer coat has broad leather shoulder panels, a heavy fur-lined collar, and rough asymmetrical edges. The lower body consists of wide black canvas trousers reinforced with thick leather around the knees and tucked into oversized black work boots. A massive leather belt with metal rings, heavy gloves, wrist wraps, and several simple forged-metal tools complete the outfit. COLOR PALETTE: dark brown, charcoal, black, natural fur, forged steel.
