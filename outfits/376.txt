This contemporary streetwear outfit coordinates a luminous midnight blue fluid satin top with structured matte black utility trousers, creating a high-contrast, modern utility-glam aesthetic. The upper body features an intentionally oversized, relaxed-fit midnight blue satin button-up shirt worn loosely unbuttoned down to the mid-torso, creating a deep, dramatic plunging V-neckline that exposes the chest and collarbones. The long sleeves are casually rolled up to the elbows, showing structured, slightly padded shoulders that define the top line. Instead of a draped panel, the high waist is cinched by a rugged, technical black webbed belt with a polished silver cobra buckle. The lower body is dominated by high-rise, structured matte black cargo trousers with a relaxed, straight-leg silhouette and minimalist flat utility pockets along the thighs, ending in clean hems that break softly over the feet. Footwear consists of chunky, square-toe black leather ankle boots with a thick, rugged platform sole. Wearable accessories are streamlined into a singular, bold metallic silver torque choker neck ring and a stack of minimalist silver bangles on the left wrist.
