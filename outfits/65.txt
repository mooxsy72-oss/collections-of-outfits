This casual summer look features a dominant, high-waisted bright red midi skirt made of lightweight, fluid woven rayon or viscose that drapes softly down the legs. It features a dense, small-scale print of scattered white wildflowers and a dramatic thigh-high side slit exposing the leg. Tucked into the waistband is a fitted, cropped white cotton t-shirt with a classic crew neckline and short sleeves, leaving the midriff covered. Slouched loosely over the arms is an oversized, bulky cream ribbed-knit cardigan with dropped shoulders, wide sleeves, and large dark brown buttons. The footwear consists of chunky white leather sneakers with a thick platform rubber sole and matching white laces, worn over low-cut white socks.
