This Victorian-era tailored walking suit features a rich palette dominated by deep plum-purple wool-satin, accented with matte black and dark brown leather, providing complete full-body coverage. The upper garment is a tightly fitted, structured hip-length plum jacket featuring notched lapels, princess seams, a pointed V-shaped front hem, small dark buttons down the center front, slightly puffed shoulders, and slim full-length sleeves with turn-back buttoned cuffs. Beneath it layers a high-collared black blouse with ruffled neck and wrist trims, finished at the throat with a layered black jabot cravat and oval cameo brooch. The matching ankle-length plum bustle skirt features heavily draped horizontal front swag folds, hip swags, and rear fullness. Glossy black lace-up Victorian heeled boots and fitted dark brown leather gloves complete the look.
