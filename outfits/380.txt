OUTFIT: contemporary outfit features a monochrome-inspired palette of dark chocolate brown and black, arranged in a highly structured, layered composition. Visual attention is drawn to the exaggerated crop of the outerwear and the volumetric, pleated lower-body elements.The base layer consists of a black, form-fitting upper-body garment that provides complete coverage with a high neckline, serving as a dark backdrop for the outerwear. Over this is an ultra-cropped, dark brown leather or faux-leather jacket with long, slim sleeves, a slight stand collar, and a clean, straight-edged front that stays open to reveal the base layer.The lower body features a matching dark brown leather mini-skirt with high-waisted construction and a dramatic pleated, flared silhouette that creates a structured, architectural volume. Beneath the skirt, form-fitting dark brown leggings or tights extend down the legs, offering continuous, sleek coverage.The footwear consists of dramatic, slouchy knee-high boots made of smooth black leather. These boots feature an oversized, voluminous shaft that folds dynamically around the lower leg, tapering into a sharp, pointed toe and a tall, slender stiletto heel, adding substantial visual weight to the bottom of the ensemble.
