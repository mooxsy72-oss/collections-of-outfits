OUTFIT: This contemporary outfit features a monochrome-inspired palette of dark chocolate brown and black, arranged in a highly structured, layered composition. Visual attention is drawn to the exaggerated crop of the outerwear and the volumetric, pleated lower-body elements. The base layer consists of a black, form-fitting upper-body garment that provides complete coverage with a high neckline, serving as a dark backdrop for the outerwear. Over this is an ultra-cropped, dark brown leather or faux-leather jacket with long, slim sleeves, a slight stand collar, and a clean, straight-edged front that stays open to reveal the base layer. The lower body features a matching dark brown leather mini-skirt with high-waisted construction and a dramatic pleated, flared silhouette that creates a structured, architectural volume. Beneath the skirt, form-fitting dark brown leggings or tights extend down the legs, offering continuous, sleek coverage. The footwear consists of elegant, knee-high black leather boots featuring a relaxed, slouchy shaft that forms soft, dynamic folds around the lower leg. The boots have a smooth, semi-glossy finish, a clean pointed toe, and a refined, mid-height heel, grounding the style in a polished and sophisticated look.
