This late Victorian mourning-inspired two-piece tailored ensemble is entirely monochromatic jet black, leaving only the hands bare. The upper garment is a tightly fitted, structured basque bodice crafted from heavy matte-sheen black floral damask jacquard with subtle tonal botanical patterns. It features a high standing mandarin collar, a concealed center-front placket, vertical boned princess seams, and an exaggeratedly cinched waist flaring into a pointed front hem with pleated hip peplums. Voluminous leg-of-mutton sleeves with puffed, pleated shoulders narrow sharply into slim, wrist-length forearms. Dominating the lower silhouette is a floor-length, full A-line skirt in matching black damask jacquard, accented near the lower hem by three parallel horizontal bands of plush black velvet ribbon. Black footwear sits hidden beneath the hem.
