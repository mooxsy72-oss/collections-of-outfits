This summery, cottagecore-inspired outfit features a single, dominant maxi-length dress crafted from a lightweight, fluid cream-colored woven fabric with a matte texture. The dress features a close-fitting bodice with a wide square neckline that leaves the collarbones exposed, transitioning into voluminous, gathered elbow-length puff sleeves with elasticized cuffs. A defined waistline flows into a sweeping, high-volume A-line skirt covered in a medium-density, small-scale black polka-dot pattern. The skirt features a dramatic thigh-high front slit that fully exposes the left leg when in motion. Grounding the silhouette are minimalist, dark brown or black heeled sandals featuring thin, delicate toe bands and slender ankle straps.
