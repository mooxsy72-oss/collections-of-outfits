This fantasy bridal gown combines Victorian silhouettes with modern ethereal textures, rendered in a monochrome palette of stark white and ivory. It features a heavily structured, rigid corset bodice with vertical boning channels, a sweetheart neckline, a pointed dropped waist, and a delicate jacquard floral texture. An attached high-neck lace collar creates a keyhole cutout over the upper chest. Detachable cold-shoulder bishop sleeves feature gathered fabric plissé at the biceps, flowing into voluminous sheer chiffon, and finishing with tight lace cuffs. The floor-length skirt consists of an open tulle overskirt with scalloped floral lace borders, parting to reveal a full, sweeping pleated tulle underskirt with detailed hemline embroidery. The outfit leaves the shoulders and upper chest partially exposed, completing the look with matching white patent leather high-heeled pumps.
