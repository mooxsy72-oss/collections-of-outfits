OUTFIT: This compact agricultural work ensemble centers on a dark forest-green canvas bomber jacket with a slightly oversized silhouette, dropped shoulders, ribbed collar and cuffs, sturdy front zipper, and multiple functional pockets. Beneath it is a thick charcoal fleece pullover with a high neck. The lower body consists of loose black-brown utility trousers with articulated knees, large thigh pockets, and a straight tapered leg. Heavy dark-brown leather boots with thick soles anchor the outfit. Black knit gloves and a rugged cross-body canvas tool bag complete the practical silhouette.
