This Central Asian nomadic and Mongolian-inspired winter ensemble features a rich palette dominated by deep chocolate brown, russet fur, crisp ivory white, and cream shearling, accented with metallic gold and silver, providing full body coverage except for the hands. Dominating the upper silhouette is an oversized, knee-length dark brown leather coat (deel) with a plush russet fur shawl collar, front placket, and hem border, paired with exaggeratedly voluminous, bulky cream-gray curly shearling lower sleeves. Worn open over a fitted, knee-length chocolate-brown silk tunic patterned with dense gold floral brocade, silver-piped mandarin collar, diagonal front closure, and a wrapped white silk sash belt. Beneath falls a flowing, floor-length ivory-white pleated skirt with gold floral motifs along the hem. Oversized silver medallion earrings with long white pearl-bead tassels complete the look.
