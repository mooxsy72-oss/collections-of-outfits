OUTFIT: ensemble reflects a traditional Slavic or Eastern European fantasy folk aesthetic, characterized by its ornamental geometric embroidery, wide woven sash, and crisscrossed leg leg-wraps. The visual composition balances a neutral, light beige base with rich secondary colors of indigo blue and deep red, creating striking focal points along the embroidered borders and the heavily textured waistline.The upper-body base is a loose-fitting, thigh-length tunic made of a lightweight, subtly textured off-white linen or hemp fabric. It features a high, split-neck collar and straight, full-length sleeves. Vibrant red geometric cross-stitch embroidery frames the neck opening, cuffs, and straight bottom hem. Over this sits a thigh-length, sleeveless open vest crafted from medium-weight indigo blue wool with a rough, slightly frayed texture. The vest is adorned with a decorative red and blue geometric trim running continuously along its entire front opening and straight bottom edge.For the lower body, voluminous, loose-fitting trousers in matching indigo blue are gathered tightly below the knees. The lower legs are wrapped securely from calf to ankle in light beige linen cloths, crisscrossed tightly with thin brown leather laces to create a compressed, textured silhouette that balances the billowing trousers.Accessories include a prominent, wide red woven textile sash wrapped multiple times around the waist, finished with long decorative fringes hanging down the left side. A small, rounded brown leather pouch hangs from this sash. Around the neck sits a simple cord necklace with a round, metallic medallion pendant. The footwear consists of sturdy, ankle-high brown leather shoes with a rounded toe and distressed, wrinkled texture.
