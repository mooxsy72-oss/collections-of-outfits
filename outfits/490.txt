OUTFIT: This cold winter barn-work outfit combines a long dark brown quilted coat with a soft oversized silhouette, high collar, large patch pockets, and thick matte insulation. Beneath it is a heavy cream cable-knit sweater layered over a dark thermal shirt. The lower body consists of straight charcoal wool trousers with reinforced knees and slightly gathered ankles. Sturdy black leather winter boots with thick soles provide traction on icy ground. Brown fleece-lined gloves and a wide leather utility belt complete the outfit. The muted colors and heavy natural textures create a warm, traditional rural winter aesthetic.
