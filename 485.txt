OUTFIT: This cold-weather agricultural outfit is built around oversized insulated bib overalls in dark brown waterproof canvas, featuring a large chest pocket, adjustable straps, reinforced knees, and generous leg volume for layering. Beneath them is a thick forest-green fleece pullover with a high collar. A short charcoal quilted jacket provides additional warmth without restricting movement. The feet are protected by tall black rubber-lined winter boots with thick treaded soles. Heavy knitted mittens and a small leather utility pouch complete the ensemble. The silhouette is bulky, practical, and optimized for bending, lifting, and outdoor farm work.
