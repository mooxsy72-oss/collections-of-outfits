OUTFIT: This practical autumn dacha outfit is built around insulated work overalls rather than a conventional jacket-and-trouser combination. The dominant garment is a pair of high-waisted padded bib overalls in deep charcoal canvas, featuring wide adjustable shoulder straps, a large chest pocket, reinforced knees, side utility pockets, and a slightly loose fit allowing warm layers underneath. Beneath them is a thick burnt-orange wool sweater with a high ribbed neckline and substantial knit texture. A cropped dark brown quilted jacket is worn over the sweater, with a simple snap front and compact rounded silhouette. The footwear consists of mid-calf olive rubber boots with thick soles, while dark leather gloves and a small canvas utility pouch complete the ensemble. The silhouette is sturdy and functional, with the bib overalls providing a distinctly agricultural workwear character.
