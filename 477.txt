OUTFIT: This particularly cold autumn dacha look is centered on a heavy cropped shearling work coat in warm camel brown, featuring dense natural wool texture, a broad rounded collar, wide sleeves, leather-bound edges, and a boxy silhouette. Underneath is a fitted dark burgundy thermal mock-neck top that adds a subtle deep color accent. The lower body consists of loose charcoal wool-blend trousers with a high waist, pleated front, and slightly tapered ankles. The feet are protected by sturdy dark brown lace-up boots with thick lugged soles and reinforced leather toes. Thick cream wool socks are slightly visible above the boots, while dark leather gardening gloves complete the look. The combination of shearling, wool, and heavy leather creates a distinctly warm rural-autumn aesthetic.
