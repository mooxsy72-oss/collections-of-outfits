OUTFIT: This winter farming look features a heavy camel-brown shearling coat reaching mid-thigh, with dense natural wool lining, broad shoulders, oversized sleeves, a high collar, and large leather-edged pockets. Beneath it is a fitted dark burgundy thermal sweater. The lower body consists of loose dark green wool trousers tucked into sturdy brown leather winter boots with thick insulated soles. Cream wool socks peek slightly above the boots. Thick brown leather gloves and a simple canvas waist pouch complete the outfit. The combination of shearling, wool, and leather creates a warm, rugged rural silhouette.
