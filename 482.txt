OUTFIT: This modern rural-work ensemble is designed for active outdoor labor in cold autumn weather. The dominant upper layer is a thick dark forest-green fleece jacket with a high neck, full front zipper, slightly oversized shoulders, elastic cuffs, and contrasting smooth nylon panels reinforcing the chest and elbows. Beneath it is a fitted dark grey thermal shirt. The lower body consists of loose tan utility trousers made from heavy brushed cotton, featuring multiple reinforced pockets, articulated knees, a high waist, and a slightly tapered ankle. The footwear consists of sturdy black hiking-work boots with thick rubber soles and reinforced toes. Dark green knit gloves and a compact black belt pouch provide functional accessories. The silhouette is athletic, practical, and mobile rather than heavily bulky.
